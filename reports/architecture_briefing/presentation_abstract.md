# NeuroBOLT architecture and a controlled next experiment

NeuroBOLT estimates regional blood-oxygen-level-dependent (BOLD) signals from preceding scalp EEG. This presentation explains the implemented model from its input tensors through temporal and spectral encoding, pooling, additive fusion and regression. It distinguishes EEG-pretrained initialization from paired BOLD supervision, and spectral magnitude from the log-power features used by a strong ridge baseline.

Completed five-fold evaluation across 22 people gives mean temporal correlations of .381 for ridge, .336 for shared 24-second NeuroBOLT and .323 for its 16-second version. These development results motivate a demanding comparator without establishing a universal information ceiling. A separate 27-person feature-cache study and exploratory NATVIEW transfer analysis highlight amplitude compression, global-versus-regional information and measurement-contract limitations. Their scores concern different endpoints and are kept separate.

UnEBOLT, UniEFS and MARBLE illustrate regional conditioning, fMRI-derived priors and token-preserving readouts. Their differing targets, training resources and evaluation protocols prevent a cross-paper ranking. The recommended two-week investigation instead changes one component: replace NeuroBOLT's dense spectral window maps with fixed support-overlap maps on a physical lag grid. Matched baselines and parameter controls distinguish predictive benefit from extra flexibility or regularization. Evaluation combines person-level raw predictive R², correlation, amplitude and coverage.

A separate exploratory global-BOLD cache pilot completed on M3: 12 model-fold fits across 27 people, with 10 checks passed. Ridge reached raw predictive R²=.0651, compared with -.0316 for a compact mean-pool transformer and -.0159 for learned pooling. The pooling difference remains uncertain, and this proxy cannot validate the proposed NeuroBOLT modification. Public paired recordings support the project; prospective Emotiv EEG could support later representation or robustness work. The final academic contribution is an auditable architecture explanation, one falsifiable experiment and a justified decision about the next research direction.
