# Build an introductory EEG honours project

**Recommendation: offer one introductory CS/AI honours project comparing spectral logistic regression with a tiny transformer on one public DREAM EEG subset.** The student should learn to audit data, prevent leakage, explain models and communicate an honest result. The Zhang–Wamsley subset offers a Monash-associated starting point with **28 people and 308 catalogued cases**, subject to ingestion, quality and label checks. An EEG-only study is complete without fMRI; additional students can extend the shared pipeline after the baseline works. If measured BOLD prediction is essential, the existing compact ds006040 cache provides the fastest continuity route. Neither neural superiority nor a clinical discovery is required for success. ([DREAM catalogue v9](https://bridges.monash.edu/articles/dataset/The_DREAM_database/22133105/9), [selected subset](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Zhang_Wamsley_2019_Final/22226692))

*Opportunity brief, 26 October 2026. Dataset/resource facts are distinguished below from proposed methods and budgets. No models, new cluster workloads, raw-data downloads or external contacts were undertaken for this synthesis.*

## One public EEG question gives the student a complete project

Ask: **“Do small EEG models predict reported sleep experience in unseen people, and how much does sleep stage explain their performance?”** This preserves NeuroBOLT's interest in learning from brain signals while reducing the initial signal-processing and supervision burden. Its contribution is an understandable, reproducible comparison with a careful confounding analysis. The supervisor must confirm that this scope meets the student's actual honours assessment requirements.

DREAM connects the project to Monash's sleep and consciousness research network. The selected **Zhang & Wamsley 2019 Final** package was collected at **Furman University**, rather than Monash. Its public version 1 declares **CC BY 4.0**, 58 EEG electrodes and 400 Hz acquisition. Labels are **0, no experience, and 2, experience**; the study did not separately elicit experience without recall. Classifying these reports cannot establish dream-content decoding or the presence or absence of consciousness. The 28/308 catalogue counts are **not raw-recording QC-verified usable sample counts**. ([Package documentation and licence](https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles/22226692), [Monash-associated DREAM paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12350935/))

The first feasibility gate is a small data audit: reconcile participant/case IDs, report labels, usable pre-awakening duration, channels, reference, artifacts and **actual sleep-stage availability**. The package warns that intended stages encoded in filenames can disagree with the final scored epoch, including wake before a delayed report marker. It also says released EEG needs an appropriate reference. Inspect actual stage annotations and marker timing; do not derive ground truth from filenames. Tabulate report categories by stage and person before choosing the analysis window. ([Subset's stage and preprocessing notes](https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles/22226692))

Proceed only if retained cases support grouped evaluation and a meaningful assessment of stage confounding. If labels cannot be separated from stage, report that limitation and agree a narrower question. If ingestion or label support obstructs the starter, switch to **Sleep-EDF cassette sleep staging**: one EEG derivation, five classes with stages 3/4 merged, documented exclusions and both nights of each person kept together. This changes the research question explicitly. ([Public Sleep-EDF release](https://physionet.org/content/sleep-edfx/1.0.0/), [MNE's reproducible tutorial](https://mne.tools/stable/auto_tutorials/clinical/60_sleep.html))

## Two small models and strong controls keep the comparison teachable

The following is a **proposed protocol**, to freeze after feasibility checks. Use one fixed pre-awakening window and a small, documented set of log-band-power features across time bins. Begin with channel averages over a declared montage; record band edges, units and reference. Logistic regression uses the flattened features; the transformer receives the same information as ordered time-bin tokens. This makes the model comparison easier to interpret than changing features and architecture together. Reproduce a limited part of DREAM's original analysis first, while independently checking its suitability for unseen-person evaluation. ([Authors' analysis release](https://zenodo.org/records/15234846))

Use a training-majority predictor and a **stage-only classifier** as controls. Where several people supply both report categories within a stage, add a prespecified within-stage analysis. If stage annotations or class overlap are inadequate, an overall classification score cannot support an experience-specific interpretation. Keep all awakenings and derived windows from one person in the same partition. Fit imputation, scaling, class weights and model selection on training people; inner validation selects settings and stopping. Freeze outer participant folds before fitting and inspect their label support without selecting them for favourable scores.

A teaching configuration is one transformer encoder layer, width 32, two heads, a scalar classification head and **fewer than 20,000 trainable parameters**, with the actual count recorded. Fix one neural seed, at most 60 epochs and patience 10. Compare mean pooling with learned time-bin weighting only as **one optional ablation**; pooling weights are not physiological explanations. With three outer folds, three logistic regularization values and one inner validation partition per fold, the main comparison requires nine EEG logistic candidates, three transformer fits and optionally three pooling-variant fits, plus the inexpensive majority/stage controls. Reduce scope before outer evaluation if the design is unreliable.

Report balanced accuracy, macro F1, confusion matrices and class support, using a declared person-balanced weighting rule. Bootstrap people, retaining each person's cases together; explain the exploratory intervals and shared training sets. Report AUROC only where both labels support it. Include failures and undefined metrics. A transparent tie or loss to logistic regression is useful evidence. Profile one validation-only fit before agreeing an initial **24 GPU-hour ceiling** on M3: this is a proposed spending limit, **not an estimated requirement, allocation or promised runtime**. No architecture search, foundation-model pretraining or automatic pursuit of better test scores belongs in the core.

## Existing paired assets offer continuity when BOLD is essential

The alternative is one offline **EEG-to-global-BOLD regression** task, using the existing ds006040 features, fixed participant folds and a training-mean/ridge baseline before one small neural comparator. BOLD is a blood-oxygenation signal; predicting its time course is a different task from classifying sleep reports. The public ds006040 release contains **28 people**; the compact study retained **27 people and 54 eyes-closed/eyes-open runs**. These denominators describe different resources. ([Public release](https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds006040/versions/1.0.2), [local study inventory](<C:/Users/Seamus/Documents/GitHub/NeuroBOLT/reports/thesis_exploration/data/manifest_inventory.json>))

The local **features_causal.npz is 3,756,893 bytes, approximately 3.8 MB**, and is ignored by Git. A fresh clone therefore needs an explicitly arranged, permitted cache handover with its manifest and hash. It supplies 300 EEG features and paired targets; select the global target by label. Reuse the loader/scorer logic, protocol, QC record and numerical exports rather than rebuilding MRI preprocessing. The older frozen demo models accept different feature dimensions and cannot be substituted directly. ([Cache](<C:/Users/Seamus/Documents/GitHub/NeuroBOLT/reports/thesis_exploration/data/features_causal.npz>), [reuse audit](<C:/Users/Seamus/Documents/GitHub/NeuroBOLT/research_notes/Monash honours EEG project/Reuse and learning scope.md>))

Historical results already explored these data: regional ridge achieved **r≈0.189, predictive R²≈0.028**, while the distinct global endpoint achieved **r≈0.277, R²≈0.065**. They are preliminary development results, not new untouched tests or future student outcomes. Past-only features inherit retrospective cleaning and target processing. Any display is a research replay; show error and amplitude in common target units alongside correlation. A new split does not restore historical independence. ([Existing scores and limitations](<C:/Users/Seamus/Documents/GitHub/NeuroBOLT/research_notes/Clinical EEG fMRI thesis exploration/M3 experiments.md>))

DREAM supplies no paired BOLD targets. An EEG encoder extension must learn and evaluate the EEG-to-BOLD mapping on paired recordings. Defer full NeuroBOLT/UnEBOLT/UniEFS/MARBLE reproduction, NATVIEW transfer and clinical deployment. Separate reuse is limited to inspected NeuroBOLT assets and attributed public code; **no sibling-project assets or permissions are assumed**.

## Grow the team around shared outputs and student understanding

One student owns the complete small pipeline. With three students, A owns ingestion/QC and split manifests, B owns models, and C owns evaluation, plots and handover. A fourth owns either the frozen pooling audit or the viewer. Everyone explains the shared experiment and has an assessable individual contribution. The supervisor confirms whether this arrangement fits separate honours assessment.

Use simple interfaces: the loader returns features plus `person_id, case_id, stage, label, split`; models implement `fit(train, validation, config)` and `predict(features)`; all emit `fold, model, seed, person_id, case_id, y_true, probability`. One scorer consumes those rows. The viewer reads saved predictions and never fits models or changes metrics. Integrate a tiny fixture early; version manifests and configurations together.

Treat **12 weeks as an illustrative first phase**, not the official course duration. Weeks 1–4 settle feasibility, splits, feature plots, majority/stage controls and logistic regression. Weeks 5–8 implement the tiny transformer, profile compute, freeze the comparison and evaluate once. Weeks 9–12 examine failures, complete permitted optional work and package a reproducible report, viewer and presentation. The student's actual course calendar and deadlines remain unknown. ([Monash honours process](https://www.monash.edu/it/current-students/courses/honours-and-minor-thesis))

Require teach-backs: explain one EEG window and label; demonstrate participant leakage; explain regularization, attention and pooling; trace one score to source rows; diagnose a small bug unaided. For the paired route, add haemodynamic timing and correlation versus predictive R². AI can explain concepts, draft small functions and help debug or edit, with an assistance log and student review. It must not select claims, repeatedly optimize on outer scores or conduct unbounded autonomous research. Follow the unit's actual AI policy and authorized data-use arrangements.

**Next student actions:** prepare the dataset/label audit plan, reproduce one small analysis, check eResearch logistics and draft the one-page question. **Next supervisor actions:** confirm course fit and assessment, identify a sleep adviser, approve the data/ethics route, arrange individual compute access and choose DREAM or the paired-cache alternative. Agree feasibility and budget gates before training. No contacts have been sent. The kickoff decision is whether this bounded comparison offers the right learning experience; stronger models and additional datasets earn their place only after the student can explain the baseline.
