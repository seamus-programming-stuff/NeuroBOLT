# Clinical EEG to fMRI feasibility

**Yes: current EEG-to-fMRI models genuinely predict part of the measured BOLD signal, including regional dynamics in people absent from training. They have not established accurate, patient-specific regional or whole-brain fMRI replacement.** Verified 2026 methods extend prediction to 64–512 atlas regions; compelling spatial reconstruction results also exist after extensive personal training. Their scores measure different things and cannot form one accuracy leaderboard. Narrow applications, such as a validated wakefulness advisory or a specified neurofeedback intervention, can be useful without reproducing every regional fluctuation. FDA-cleared Prism provides a real precedent for adjunctive PTSD biofeedback, explicitly excluding diagnostic interpretation. Accuracy is limited by a combination of incomplete measurements, variable physiology, target noise and inadequate evaluation; their respective contributions remain unmeasured. No established scaling law specifies how many paired people or parameters would deliver clinical substitution, so investment should follow staged experiments and indication-specific validation.

*Evidence cutoff: 21 October 2026. Project reports and analyses are exploratory, not independently peer-reviewed evidence. Resource ranges are engineering proposals, not measured requirements or regulatory minimums.*

## New models recover real signals, but their scores answer different questions

“Recreate fMRI” needs an operational definition. **Temporal correlation** asks whether an estimated region rises and falls when its measured BOLD does. **Spatial correlation** asks whether one predicted brain pattern resembles the corresponding measured pattern across locations. Structural similarity, or SSIM, measures image resemblance; functional-connectivity correlation compares relationships between regions. These are useful, distinct outcomes. Correct connectivity does not identify the original trajectory: applying the same time permutation to every regional trace preserves its connectivity while destroying alignment with measured events.

## Missing observations and correctable errors require different experiments

EEG records mixtures of electrical currents; BOLD reflects haemodynamics influenced by neural activity, metabolism, vascular properties and systemic physiology. These overlap without being interchangeable. A mathematical EEG inverse ambiguity matters only when indistinguishable inputs correspond to **different values of the chosen BOLD target**. It does not prove that a global state or constrained regional response is unpredictable.

The relevant error decomposition makes the investment decision explicit. Let \(X\) contain exactly the information available at deployment—EEG history, preprocessing, allowed physiology and any personal calibration—and let \(Y\) be a specified measured BOLD value. Under squared error and finite variance, define \(m(X)=\mathbb E[Y\mid X]\). Then

\[
\mathbb E[(Y-f(X))^2]
=\underbrace{\mathbb E[\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid X)]}_{\text{remaining uncertainty given these inputs}}
+\underbrace{\mathbb E[(m(X)-f(X))^2]}_{\text{model/estimation error}}.
\]

The best average answer for an observed EEG history need not be the actual BOLD value on that occasion. More training data or a better architecture can reduce the second term. Additional informative measurements can reduce the first by changing \(X\). **A large residual alone does not reveal which term dominates.** For nonconstant \(m(X)\), the oracle population identity \(r_{\max}^2=\operatorname{Var}(m(X))/\operatorname{Var}(Y)\) describes an input-specific correlation limit; it supplies no numerical ceiling without estimating those quantities. These are mathematical deductions, not empirical project estimates.

A measurable **fMRI noise ceiling** is a separate question. If genuinely repeated measurements observe the same underlying signal, \(Y_1=S+e_1\), \(Y_2=S+e_2\), with independent equal-variance errors independent of \(S\), reliability is \(\rho=\operatorname{Corr}(Y_1,Y_2)\), and the correlation ceiling for predicting \(S\) against one noisy measurement is \(\sqrt\rho\). Repeated stimuli can estimate repeatable task responses; two resting visits do not repeat identical spontaneous trajectories. Multi-echo measurements require an explicit correlated-noise model. Neither reliability nor its square root measures EEG observability. ([Lage-Castellanos et al., 2019](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1006397))

Report temporal r alongside held-out predictive \(R^2=1-\mathrm{SSE}/\mathrm{SST}\), specifying the baseline, amplitude calibration and aggregation. Correlation ignores positive rescaling and offsets; predictive R² can be negative. **Mean(r)² is neither calibrated R² nor a fraction of neural variance.** A diffusion sample can represent a possible outcome, but photorealism cannot certify that a patient's pathological fluctuation occurred. Conversely, reproducing actual BOLD is not automatically reproducing neural function when vascular coupling is abnormal.

## Narrow clinical usefulness already exists; diagnostic substitution needs new evidence

The stakeholder explanation is straightforward: **EEG provides useful clues about brain state. A model can turn those clues into a useful estimate or intervention without measuring everything an fMRI scan measures. A realistic image cannot establish an individual patient's missing detail.** Choose the clinical action first, then validate the signal that supports it.

Prism/Amyg-EFP illustrates that distinction. FDA **K222101**, cleared on 17 March 2023, covers EEG biofeedback for relaxation/stress reduction and adjunctive PTSD symptom treatment under professional supervision alongside other interventions. It explicitly excludes diagnostic conclusions, classification and clinical interpretation. Its supporting prospective, open-label, single-arm study involved five sites: 79 full-analysis patients, 66 in the efficacy set and 63 per protocol. At three months, 66.67% of the efficacy set met a ≥6-point CAPS-5 improvement endpoint. This is **clearance, not approval of diagnostic fMRI synthesis**; the uncontrolled study cannot isolate treatment efficacy. Its journal report and regulatory summary describe the same underlying trial, not two replications. ([FDA decision/summary](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf22/K222101.pdf); [Fruchter et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.psychres.2023.115711))

Related randomized proof-of-concept work in 59 adults with PTSD compared trauma-script feedback, neutral feedback and no neurofeedback, with unresolved exposure/placebo contributions. Thus controlled clinical research exists, although it does not establish whole-brain replacement or settle every therapeutic mechanism. ([Fruchtman-Steinbok et al., 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8551212/))

For this project's **wakefulness advisory**, the intended action could be a technologist reviewing sustained loss of wakefulness during a specified adult scan. Validate against blinded sleep scoring, independent pupil/eyelid measurements and behavioural responses as appropriate. Because EEG-derived sleep labels share an input modality, document scoring independence and seek corroborating non-EEG evidence. Report event sensitivity, **false alarms/hour, delay relative to independently labelled event onset**, alert probability calibration, monitoring coverage and missing-data failures. Global-BOLD correlation does not validate drowsiness alerts; arousal is also not interchangeable with motion or unusable imaging. Any claim of improved acquisition additionally needs blinded scan-quality assessment and prospective workflow outcomes.

For **regional feedback**, validate the named target's sign, amplitude, lag and between-day agreement against real paired fMRI, including physiology/task-only controls. Then test symptoms or function, durability and harms against an appropriate comparator. A therapeutic effect and faithful ROI reconstruction are separate claims: benefit can occur through a useful surrogate without validating a complete biological account.

For **functional-imaging replacement**, choose one decision—such as specified language lateralization—before discussing a broad scan substitute. Count wrong-sided decisions, missed critical sites, localization errors and abstentions against independent clinical evidence and appropriate follow-up. Actual fMRI is an imperfect comparator; presurgical mapping and postoperative outcomes answer additional questions. Whole-brain replacement bundles many regional and disease-specific claims. Keep anatomical MRI and standard escalation pathways: an atlas-painted prediction supplies no individual lesion anatomy. ([AAN presurgical fMRI guideline](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5272968/); [IMDRF clinical evaluation](https://www.imdrf.org/sites/default/files/docs/imdrf/final/technical/imdrf-tech-170921-samd-n41-clinical-evaluation_1.pdf))

The internal work supports baselines and questions, not stronger clinical claims. Subject-grouped VU CV reported seven-target ridge R **.381** versus shared 24-s NeuroBOLT **.336**; removing global signal changed 64-region mean R **.343 to .144**. That motivates regional-residual tests without proving that the removed prediction was artifact. Frozen ridge on 28 ds006040 people gave eyes-closed .227 versus .228 trained within site and eyes-open .140. Those estimates do not establish equivalence, the cause of lower accuracy or general clinical portability. The small VU learning curve does not show that more people cannot help; null nonlinear/calibration results do not establish universal linear sufficiency. ([Internal interpretability report](<C:/Users/Seamus/Documents/GitHub/NeuroBOLT/reports/NeuroBOLT interpretability.md>); [internal external-validation report](<C:/Users/Seamus/Documents/GitHub/NeuroBOLT/reports/NeuroBOLT external validation.md>))

The prototype's streaming-replay global-BOLD **r .263** is likewise exploratory. It replays already cleaned EEG, rather than prospectively recording and cleaning live scanner input. Its approximately **3 ms of computation per fMRI volume** is not biological detection latency or validated clinical alarm delay. It uses **24 s of history** and predicts a haemodynamically delayed target; 24 s of context is not itself a fixed alarm-delay estimate. End-to-end timing must measure acquisition, buffering, artifact correction, event definition and decision persistence. The earlier reports' “live” and “ceiling” wording should therefore not carry into clinical claims.

The project's approximately 2,040-parameter shared-kernel model is another internal engineering baseline, not evidence that all useful mappings are that small. A giant transformer is not a demonstrated prerequisite. Unpaired fMRI pretraining can improve a decoder prior, as UniEFS demonstrates, but does not itself validate EEG-to-BOLD correspondence in patients. For unrestricted whole-brain replacement, **neither required N nor sufficient model capacity is currently supportable**. Clinical arousal-label cohorts and EEG-only therapeutic follow-up need not all add paired MRI hours; their references and costs must be budgeted separately.

Training sufficiency and clinical validation precision must be budgeted separately. For a frozen binary decision, independent reference-positive cases and an illustrative two-sided 95% normal approximation give

\[
n_+=\left\lceil\frac{1.96^2\,Se(1-Se)}{d^2}\right\rceil.
\]

| Arithmetic assumptions, not observed performance | Independent class denominator | Expected accrual at assumed 10% positivity |
|---|---|---|
| Sensitivity .90, half-width .05 | **139 reference-positive people/events** | **1,390** before loss/clustering |
| Sensitivity .90, half-width .03 | **385 positives** | **3,850** before loss/clustering |
| Specificity .95, half-width .03 | **203 reference-negative people/events** | Check negative yield separately |

Here positivity could mean a prespecified sustained wakefulness-loss episode or one affected scan/person; **10% is an assumed endpoint prevalence, not disease epidemiology**. At 15% loss, the first expected recruitment becomes \(\lceil1390/.85\rceil=1,636\). Expected accrual is not assurance of the required positive count. These approximations need final Wilson/exact-interval planning; estimating near 90% does not prove sensitivity exceeds 90%. **139 is not a device-specific regulatory minimum**, and Prism's therapeutic endpoint requires different calculations. ([Buderer, 1996](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8870764/); [FDA diagnostic reporting guidance](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/statistical-guidance-reporting-results-studies-evaluating-diagnostic-tests-guidance-industry-and-fda))

Patients or distinct events are the units, not overlapping windows. Illustratively, ten positive events/person with within-person ICC .20 give design effect \(1+9(.20)=2.8\): about 390 observed events approximate 139 independent events, without establishing adequate patient diversity. Site clustering needs its own design; neither a generic inflation factor nor more familiar-site windows addresses systematic site shift. Under assumed sensitivity .90/specificity .95, PPV is **66.7% at 10% prevalence** and **15.4% at 1%**. Continuous monitoring therefore needs actual false alarms/hour and event-delay distributions. ([Cluster-design methods](https://academic.oup.com/ije/article/44/3/1051/632956))

Prospective external tests should use a frozen pipeline, new patients, future acquisition periods and new sites; provisionally plan 4–6 external sites for the narrow programme, then size denominators from pilot prevalence and dependence. Count failures and abstentions across everyone enrolled. Therapeutic utility needs an appropriate controlled outcome study: assuming independent equal arms, alpha .05, 80% power and standardized effect .5–.3 gives approximately **126–350 analyzable patients**, an illustration rather than an EEG-fMRI efficacy estimate. Presurgical replacement needs agreed error/noninferiority margins and discordance estimates before sizing. Zero harmful events in 300 independent cases only bounds the underlying risk at approximately 1% (one-sided 95%); tighter safety margins require more cases. TGA/IMDRF require evidence appropriate to purpose; they supply no universal EEG-fMRI R or participant threshold. ([TGA AI evidence guidance](https://www.tga.gov.au/products/medical-devices/software-and-artificial-intelligence-ai/manufacturing/artificial-intelligence-ai-and-medical-device-software-regulation/evidence-requirements-software-using-ai); [IMDRF N41](https://www.imdrf.org/sites/default/files/docs/imdrf/final/technical/imdrf-tech-170921-samd-n41-clinical-evaluation_1.pdf))

## M3 should resolve information value before funding broad reconstruction

**The next experiment should cross training people, minutes/person and information available at inference on fixed person/site holdouts.** Use existing VU/ds006040 only as development context; reserve any genuinely untouched release before inspecting its outcomes. MULTI-CLARID is attractive for respiration/artifact controls; ds003768 for sleep-stage/event assessment; NATVIEW for repeat visits and eye/respiratory measures. First audit usable channels, clocks, references, permissions, duplicated participants and synchronized hours. Physiological comparisons must be restricted to jointly available sensors; absent capnography cannot be inferred from a respiration belt.

Fit tuned lagged ridge, the shared spectral/lag kernel and one compact neural model on identical folds and causal context. Include physiology-only, EEG-plus-physiology, global-template and task-only controls where applicable. Separately report global BOLD, full regional traces and specified global/physiology-adjusted residual targets. Residual performance tests spatial specificity under that target definition, not a pure neural fraction. Evaluate temporal r, calibrated error/R², lag, regional contrasts and individual failures; retain spatial/FC metrics as secondary outcomes. Split before learned preprocessing; audit filter/context boundaries, normalization and future-data access. UniEFS's epoch-derived vigilance prompts, for example, require a separate causality/latency audit before online claims.

For development learning curves, hold evaluation people fixed and vary new training people, duration and capacity independently, keeping repeated visits grouped. Use nested tuning and person-level uncertainty with appropriate temporal surrogates; report site variation explicitly. Repeatedly inspected development holdouts must not become the final clinical test. A nonsignificant difference, including p>.05, is not equivalence: define a useful margin and obtain adequate precision. A nonlinear win under these controls would revise the current preference for ridge; a robust added-sensor win would prioritize acquisition rather than more parameters.

For this small team, the proposed first M3 allocation is CPU-based QC/features/ridge plus **one compact GPU experiment at a time**. Profile one fold/seed and a few capacities before requesting a larger allocation. Advancing two candidates to five development folds and three seeds would require **30 fits**; multiply by measured GPU-hours/fit instead of inventing a fixed compute budget. Log peak RAM/VRAM, preprocessing time and storage expansion. As explicit storage arithmetic, 64 channels × 5,000 samples/s × four bytes × 3,600 s is **4.61 GB per recorded EEG hour**, before compression, auxiliary channels, MRI, derivatives or backups. At the illustrative 1,500 acquired hours that is approximately **6.9 TB of EEG alone**. Actual formats and sampling change this figure; scanner bookings exceed paired minutes because preparation and failed/repeated acquisitions consume time. Obtain local combined EEG-fMRI, staffing and sensor quotes rather than extrapolating an old scanner-only rate. A 1,000-person programme would require a clinical acquisition network and dedicated coordination/QC support; access to M3 does not supply those resources.

Human investigators determine the next branch. An MR physicist and EEG specialist must validate synchronized acquisition, heating/artifact procedures and prospective causal cleaning. A sleep/arousal investigator must define events and independent pupil/sleep/behaviour references; a technologist and patients must set acceptable alert burden and actions. A psychiatrist/neurofeedback investigator is needed for therapeutic target engagement, comparators and symptom endpoints. Neurology, neuroradiology and surgical mapping collaborators must define clinically meaningful regional errors and adjudication. A biostatistician must size external tests and clustered events. New CO2/CVR manipulations, repeat-day calibration and recruitment of vascular/pathological groups require human-led protocols and ethics; scripts cannot answer those physiological questions from missing measurements.

The **go/no-go gates** should be agreed before acquisition. Advance arousal work only if causal, independently labelled predictions meet clinician-defined false-alarm, delay and coverage limits on external data; then conduct silent prospective recording followed by supervised workflow evaluation. Advance regional synthesis only if it preserves patient-specific residual contrasts and abnormal responses beyond global/physiology/simple baselines, with acceptable amplitude and lag errors across visits/sites. Expand toward 1,000+ paired people only while learning curves and clinically relevant gains justify the cost. Failure at a gate should narrow the claim or motivate a specific measurement change, not be relabelled a universal biological ceiling. Early live evaluation must assess the human–system workflow, not merely replay agreement. ([DECIDE-AI](https://www.bmj.com/content/377/bmj-2022-070904.long))

## Conclusion

The defensible investment is an information-and-validation programme with a narrow initial clinical action. What would change the recommendation is a reproduced, frozen external result preserving an individual patient's clinically relevant regional abnormalities, accompanied by calibrated uncertainty and prospective decision benefit. Strong spatial synthesis, useful neurofeedback and moderate temporal prediction are reasons to pursue that test; none substitutes for it. If independent arousal labels support a useful advisory while regional residuals remain inadequate, that is a meaningful product direction with its own evidence—not a failed attempt to manufacture a scan.
