# Clinical EEG fMRI thesis exploration

*Test what EEG reveals before translation*

**EEG-to-fMRI predicts part of healthy BOLD; diagnostic substitution remains unestablished.** M3 experiments found modest prediction and data-resource gains, with compressed amplitudes and uncertain regional specificity. NATVIEW's strict confirmation failed its input contract; a separate exploratory amendment recovered six recordings but showed weak transfer and negative regional predictive R². These results support a CS thesis on physical measurement design, missing-observation dynamics, counted calibration and selective prediction. Clinical tools require separate validation, despite a specific fMRI-informed EEG feedback intervention's FDA clearance. ## Published models predict different targets, so their numbers cannot form a leaderboard

EEG records scalp electrical activity. Functional MRI's blood-oxygen-level-dependent (BOLD) signal reflects haemodynamic changes. A region of interest (ROI) summarizes a specified brain area's time course; predicting it is narrower than reproducing a diagnostic scan. ([Physiological context](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-025-01945-y))

**UnEBOLT and UniEFS are different models.** UnEBOLT shares an EEG representation across ROI-conditioned adapters and prediction heads. UniEFS adds an fMRI-pretrained decoder and demographic, dataset and vigilance context. MARBLE combines Mamba/attention processing with ROI cross-attention. They estimate atlas-region time courses, rather than recovering an individual's anatomical scan. Their main evaluations reuse VU data, so three publications are not three independent clinical replications. ([UnEBOLT](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v315/main/assets/li26g/li26g.pdf), [UniEFS](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v306/main/assets/li26gp/li26gp.pdf), [MARBLE](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026/paper/4437_paper.pdf))

| Published evidence | People, targets and independence | Reported result and interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| **UnEBOLT, MIDL 2026** | VU: 22 healthy people/29 scans; subject-disjoint 18/5/6 train/validation/test scans; six test scans represent five people. 26 channels; preceding 16 seconds; 64 ROIs. | Temporal **r .418 ± .036**, matched NeuroBOLT .413 ± .052; FC-edge r .549 ± .120. The ± values are standard deviations, not confidence intervals. Temporal correlation measures aligned rises/falls but ignores positive rescaling and offsets. Predictive R² includes amplitude/offset errors; negatives mean worse squared error than its mean-reference baseline. It is neither squared correlation nor neural activity recovered. A common time permutation preserves connectivity while destroying event timing. Neither FC agreement nor image resemblance establishes the actual regional trajectory. These are mathematical distinctions, not allegations about the papers.

Official UnEBOLT/MARBLE source exists, but no public task-trained checkpoint was verified; LaBraM initialization is different. UniEFS still lists code/data as forthcoming. MARBLE reports 2.6 million parameters versus UnEBOLT's 8.2 million; neither establishes clinical latency. Reproduction requires locked releases, preprocessing, channel identities/order, units and atlas. **None of these three models was reproduced here.** ([Pinned UnEBOLT](https://github.com/neurdylab/UnEBOLT/tree/09a382897b85820854ddd4e47a1f851fa43d32d9), [pinned MARBLE](https://github.com/emeelkaa/marble/tree/28ae2ab16585f7a0cd57fcc961952661cce3e1d6), [UniEFS project](https://soupeeli.github.io/UniEFS/))

## Clinical evidence supports specific interventions and representations

**Prism's clearance is narrow and does not authorize diagnostic fMRI substitution.** FDA K222101 covers adjunctive PTSD biofeedback alongside other interventions under professional supervision, excluding diagnostic conclusions/classification/clinical interpretation. Its supporting five-site study was open-label and single-arm: 79 full-analysis participants, 66 in the efficacy set. Improvement in that intervention does not measure reconstruction accuracy or transfer to UnEBOLT, UniEFS or MARBLE. A registered 250-participant Prism-versus-sham trial was recruiting with no posted results at the literature audit; it must remain prospective evidence. FDA clearance also does not establish Australian authorization. ([FDA summary](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf22/K222101.pdf), [NCT06770998](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06770998))

| Application | What is supported | What remains to be demonstrated |
|---|---|---|
| Disease representation | UniEFS's frozen representation, with a fitted disease classifier, achieved PD AUC .87 in subject-level five-fold probing of 44 EEG-only people, 22 patients/22 controls. [Appendix D.5](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v306/main/assets/li26gp/li26gp.pdf). | Paired patient BOLD fidelity, differential diagnosis, external calibration and added value over direct EEG. |
| Stroke rehabilitation | A randomized programme had 30 completers: five actual bimodal EEG-fMRI plus nine EEG-only feedback sessions versus motor imagery without feedback; FMA-UE change differed, p=.048. [Butet 2025](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12984-025-01598-9). | Benefit from synthesized BOLD, any requested model, or the isolated EEG/fMRI component. |
| Stroke translation | A separate NeuroBOLT adaptation studied 13 patients and 14 controls with different-day EEG/fMRI. [Original manuscript](https://publish.illinois.edu/ritahtpeng/files/2026/01/IEEE2025_revised__Asynchronous_EEG_to_fMRI_Translation_Using_Multi_dimensional_Feature_Mapping_in_Stroke-3.pdf). | Recovery of the contemporaneous trajectory or validated longitudinal recovery prediction. |
| Arousal support | Physiological rationale and healthy reconstruction; UniEFS uses vigilance as an input. [UniEFS](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v306/main/assets/li26gp/li26gp.pdf), [brain–body coupling](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-025-01945-y). | Independent event references, added value over direct EEG, acceptable alert burden and workflow benefit. |

The distinction is consequential: **patient representation, target engagement, treatment benefit and signal fidelity are separate endpoints.** UniEFS's PD result does not validate patient BOLD recovery. Its vigilance prompt uses 63-second epochs and is not an independent alert-validation result. Similarly, the rehabilitation programme does not validate synthetic motor-network feedback. Actual simultaneous EEG-fMRI has patient localization/outcome evidence in epilepsy, but that cannot establish that an EEG-only generator reproduces clinically important maps. ([UniEFS methods](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v306/main/assets/li26gp/li26gp.pdf), [Butet](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12984-025-01598-9), [epilepsy outcome study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34400584/))

The first application should therefore be conditional: a research-grade advisory for sustained wakefulness loss during a specified adult MRI protocol, evaluated silently. Compare direct EEG vigilance, EEG-plus-physiology and inferred-BOLD representations using identical causal inputs. Require independently defined events with blinded adjudication and suitable non-EEG corroboration; pupil missingness alone is not a sleep label. Measure sensitivity, false alarms/hour, onset delay, calibration and monitored coverage. If inferred BOLD adds no useful value over direct EEG, drop that intermediary. An alternative is **one prespecified ROI/network feedback signal**, first validated against actual paired patient fMRI for sign, amplitude, lag and repeatability, then tested as an intervention. Clinical teams must choose the action and tolerances. ([TGA evidence requirements](https://www.tga.gov.au/products/medical-devices/software-and-artificial-intelligence-ai/manufacturing/artificial-intelligence-ai-and-medical-device-software-regulation/evidence-requirements-software-using-ai))

The bounded ds006040 study completed with **27 people/54 eyes-closed (EC) and eyes-open (EO) runs**, all passing QC. All nine root jobs completed with exit 0:0; ten synthetic tests and frame, group, label-budget and hash checks passed. The **96 fitted outer cells—48 per model—reuse these people, rather than representing 96 independent cohorts**. VU/ds006040 remain previously inspected development resources. The factorial crosses **4/12 training people × 120/240 retained target-seconds/person × EEG/ECG timing-and-availability/combined × tuned ridge/tiny MLP**; mixed-condition and availability controls complete the 96 cells. Three person-disjoint outer folds evaluate each person once; four separate inner validation people per fold choose hyperparameters, epochs and affine calibration. Nested early segments couple retained duration with state/content; one subset order and one neural seed cannot isolate a universal sample-count effect. At 240 retained seconds, actual target acquisition spans reach 330 seconds because of gaps.

Both M3 EEG models consume **300 lagged power features**, not raw waveforms, from the preceding 24 seconds, with training-only imputation/standardization. This does **not** establish online operation: inherited EEG cleaning, zero-phase/whole-run fMRI target processing and a mask with future margins remain retrospective. Metrics use normalized cached-target units. Training-fitted residualization uses held-out measured global BOLD only to define outcomes, never inputs; these residuals are not a pure neural fraction.

The primary panel was fixed before outcomes: EC training/test, 12 training people, 240 retained seconds/person, full regional64. Scores average regional/run metrics within each person, then people. Brackets are **95% exploratory person-bootstrap intervals**, with 2,000 resamples and paired resampling for differences. They are conditional on this bounded design, without multiplicity correction or full accounting for shared training sets. The coordinator independently checked primary means and selected contrasts. ## Three linked work packages make the thesis falsifiable

The proposed thesis asks: **which individual BOLD targets are supported by partial observations, how can a transferable dynamical estimator use that information, and when is reporting its answer justified?** EEG measures mixed electrical activity; BOLD also depends on haemodynamics and systemic state. Observational coupling does not make the modalities interchangeable. Under squared loss, finite variance and a fixed population, prediction error separates into remaining conditional variability and error in estimating the conditional mean. A fitted residual alone cannot identify their sizes. Better estimation and better measurements therefore require different experiments. ([Functional observability](https://arxiv.org/html/2201.07256), [physiological coupling](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-025-01945-y), [target-noise assumptions](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1006397))

| Package and candidate CS contribution | Decisive test | What would falsify the positive contribution claim |
|---|---|---|
| **WP1: information and measurement design.** Develop a finite-history, noise-aware diagnostic that ranks montage/history/auxiliary-sensor choices for a specified regional target under uncertain measurement operators. | Compare predicted information rankings with held-out gains against random, uniform and cost-matched measurement choices; use known-state simulations and real acquisition-domain tests. | Recommended measurements fail to outperform alternatives with adequate precision, or the diagnostic is too conservative to guide decisions. |
| **WP2: transferable structured dynamical estimation.** Learn dynamics supporting irregular/missing observations, with constrained person/visit observation and haemodynamic adaptation. | Compare cold start and counted calibration against fixed-window ridge, state/physiology priors and output-only recalibration; test later visits, regional contrasts and a frozen domain. | Gains disappear beyond global/state templates, missingness-aware estimation adds no useful coverage/skill, or constrained adaptation fails to transfer. |
| **WP3: selective prediction under stated shift assumptions.** Combine measurement diagnostics and predictive state to select reportable estimates with a scoped calibration/risk statement. | Compare accepted error at matched coverage against quality rejection, model disagreement and established calibration methods; include width and failures. | Useful coverage collapses, intervals are uninformative, or observed errors violate the claimed supported-domain criterion. |

These are **novel contribution candidates**, not first-ever claims. Functional observers and sensor design already exist; EEG-fMRI state-space fusion predates current neural decoders. A new HRF layer or renamed latent state is insufficient originality. WP2 must specify what is identifiable, under which assumptions and up to which equivalent parameterizations. Its practical contribution could be a constrained adaptation algorithm and a validated account of which parameters need personal calibration. Simulations can establish behaviour under their known operators; observational human prediction cannot by itself identify unique neural generators. ([Functional observers](https://arxiv.org/html/2201.07256), [earlier EEG-fMRI fusion](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6870602/), [state-space fusion](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2019/file/6aed000af86a084f9cb0264161e29dd3-Paper.pdf))

NATVIEW motivates a concrete WP2 experiment: compare time-aware missing-observation dynamics and uncertainty with fixed-window ridge at matched QC. First test reference harmonization on a physically shared raw montage as a controlled engineering baseline; that alone is not novel. No such extra arm ran here. Then test constrained operator/montage adaptation and counted calibration on later visits, measuring target error and risk/coverage. Respect physical gaps; resting visits neither repeat identical trajectories nor establish timepoint noise ceilings.

WP3's guarantees require explicit sampling assumptions. Complete-person conformal calibration can allow dependence within records while providing marginal coverage for a new exchangeable person; it does not certify each patient or arbitrary new sites. Covariate-shift weighting needs support overlap and unchanged conditional target law, with qualification for estimated weights. Changed neurovascular coupling can violate this without obvious EEG-input shift. Selection needs its own calibration argument; EEG-only deployment supplies no true BOLD residual feedback. NATVIEW's eight attempted/six accepted people cannot certify reliability. ([Grouped calibration](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.06342), [covariate shift](https://arxiv.org/html/1904.06019), [coverage beyond exchangeability](https://arxiv.org/html/2202.13415v5), [risk control](https://arxiv.org/html/2101.02703v3))

## Collaborators and decision gates determine the next investment

The weak secondary transfer prioritizes validated operators/references, missingness-aware dynamics and counted adaptation, rather than assuming more parameters will fix it. Freeze one primary target per package: WP1 chooses observations, WP2 estimates, WP3 selects outputs. A proposed **.02 predictive R²** gain needs prespecified precision; it is not a clinical threshold. Wide intervals are neither equivalence nor evidence of a ceiling.

Further public-data choices should follow the missing evidence. MULTI-CLARID offers richer pulse/respiration and artifact-reference controls, with corrected EEG but BOLD processing still required. Penn State ds003768 offers behavioural wakefulness button presses and eye measurements, although absent presses are not definitive sleep labels and processed-MRI timing needs resolution. NIH sleep1 offers corrected derivatives and sleep scores, but those scores depend on EEG and require careful clock alignment. None is a turnkey independently labelled clinical benchmark; do not sum nominal participants or hours into a verified usable corpus. ([MULTI-CLARID](https://github.com/OpenNeuroDatasets/ds005795/blob/a225eb4608bd72e20a6aa6aa6fdda09692f77a7f/README), [current ds003768 release](https://github.com/OpenNeuroDatasets/ds003768/tree/a3b1ccbc491ab764618bda8611311eb710ebbe38), [NIH FAQ](https://s3.amazonaws.com/openneuro.org/ds005127/FAQ))

| Human owner | Concrete responsibility and gate |
|---|---|
| Candidate and CS supervisors | Establish originality against observers, fusion, adaptation and selective inference; approve restricted theory/counterexamples and the thesis pivot if a positive hypothesis fails. |
| MR physicist and EEG specialists | Verify physical observation operators: clocks, electrode geometry, reference, units, artifacts and prospective cleaning. **Stop transfer/online claims** when these fail. |
| Sleep clinician, technologist and patient representatives | Define events, independent corroboration, action and acceptable sensitivity/false alerts/delay/coverage. **No workflow advance** without these frozen references and thresholds. |
| Neurofeedback/neurology collaborators | Choose one clinically meaningful target; validate paired patient target engagement and abnormality preservation before an intervention claim. |
| Biostatistician and institutions | Size independent people/sites/events and calibration reserves; address clustering, multiplicity, ethics, permissions, recruitment and funding. |

A public-data thesis core needs CPU QC/ridge, modest profiled GPU access, storage discipline and specialist time. Earlier **40–80-person feasibility**, **150–300-person advisory development**, **300–600-person feedback** and conditional **1,000+** brackets are acquisition proposals, not demonstrated requirements. At two 30-minute visits, 40–80 people supply 40–80 acquired paired hours; hypothetical 75% retention leaves 30–60. A 1,000-person, two-45-minute programme instead supplies 1,500 acquired hours and needs a funded acquisition network. No verified EEG-to-BOLD scaling law converts those hours or parameter counts into clinical readiness. Training diversity, external-test precision and clinical event accrual need separate budgets. ([Earlier planning assumptions](<C:/Users/Seamus/Documents/GitHub/NeuroBOLT/reports/Clinical EEG to fMRI feasibility.md>), [adjacent, not translation-specific, learning curves](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09250-1))

A realistic sequence is Year 1 measurement diagnostics and trustworthy benchmarks, Year 2 structured estimation and counted calibration, then Year 3 selection theory and frozen external audits. Clinical acquisition is conditional on collaborators and funding. Advance to silent prospective arousal recording only after a fully causal pipeline and independent references support clinician-set limits; an action study additionally requires workflow evidence. Advance ROI feedback only after patient target fidelity, then a controlled intervention study. Neither regulatory clearance nor a large patient trial should be a graduation dependency. ([TGA](https://www.tga.gov.au/products/medical-devices/software-and-artificial-intelligence-ai/manufacturing/artificial-intelligence-ai-and-medical-device-software-regulation/evidence-requirements-software-using-ai), [DECIDE-AI](https://www.bmj.com/content/377/bmj-2022-070904.long))

## Conclusion

The completed studies give the thesis a concrete starting point: training resources help within development data, while physical input contracts, continuous-history coverage and regional error constrain transfer. An original estimator or rigorous limitation result must address those observed failures under stated assumptions. Clinical usefulness still requires independently referenced target engagement or decisions; these results do not supply a reconstructed patient scan.
