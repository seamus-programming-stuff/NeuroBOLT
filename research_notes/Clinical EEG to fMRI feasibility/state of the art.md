# EEG-to-measured-fMRI reconstruction: model evidence audit

Research cutoff: **21 October 2026, Australia/Sydney**. Independently checked primary proceedings, original manuscripts, publisher/PubMed records, repositories and FDA records. This is an evidence audit, not an independent reproduction. Only this assigned notes file was added to the project; primary-source reading caches were stored outside the repository. No experiments, existing report edits, commits or pushes were undertaken. Recent inaccessible claims are retained as unverified rather than used to rank models.

## 1. Which models actually recover measured BOLD in held-out people?

### Takeaway

There is credible positive evidence for **moderate temporal recovery of measured regional BOLD in healthy people absent from training**. NeuroBOLT's selected-target result, MARBLE's 64-region result, and UniEFS's 512-region result are substantive demonstrations, but their target sets and evaluations differ. None establishes accurate recovery of all of a new patient's voxel-level fluctuations. [NeuroBOLT, Table 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.05341v2); [MARBLE, Table 1](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026/paper/4437_paper.pdf); [UniEFS, Table 1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v306/main/assets/li26gp/li26gp.pdf).

### Cited Findings

**Reading the table.** Temporal R means Pearson correlation over time between a prediction and the corresponding measured signal, unless explicitly stated otherwise. A mean over selected ROIs, a whole-atlas mean, spatial frame correlation, static-FC edge correlation, CCC and SSIM are different estimands. The table preserves the original aggregation where stated; “±” is the paper's reported SD, not a confidence interval. A held-out scan/run is not necessarily a held-out person. “No calibration” refers to the target person's measured fMRI, not the paired EEG-fMRI used to develop the model.

### Inferences

- **Strongest verified positive evidence should be presented in tiers, not a universal leaderboard.** Selected-target temporal recovery: NeuroBOLT .473. Wider atlas reconstruction: MARBLE .434 over 64 ROIs; UniEFS .367 over 512. MARBLE improves its own matched model comparison; UniEFS improves its own matched 512-ROI comparison. The narrower/easier targets, global signal inclusion and fixed splits preclude ranking those means as interchangeable. [NeuroBOLT Table 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.05341v2); [MARBLE Table 1](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026/paper/4437_paper.pdf); [UniEFS Table 1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v306/main/assets/li26gp/li26gp.pdf).
- **Strong within-person evidence is real but separate.** NeuroBOLT's .531 mean and BEIRA's .44 mean/.62 caudate example demonstrate learned temporal information after paired personal calibration. Qu et al.'s .824 is a stronger-looking number for a different, spatial metric. MEL's claimed .543/.499 remains provisional pending the baseline/split audit. None is a verified field-wide maximum. [BEIRA Table 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2211.02024); [Qu et al. Table 1](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/papers/Qu_Modeling_Spatiotemporal_Neural_Frames_for_High_Resolution_Brain_Dynamic_CVPR_2026_paper.pdf).
- **These experiments generally reconstruct BOLD at the current aligned target time from EEG history.** Testing on the final minutes of a scan does not mean predicting those minutes without observing their EEG. A six-second hemodynamic offset is not six seconds of validated forecasting beyond available neural evidence. [NeuroBOLT task formulation](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.05341v2); [CATD preprocessing and inference](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.00777).

### Gaps

- There is no common fully audited patient-level benchmark across these models, identical target cleaning, repeated subject-grouped folds, strong tuned ridge controls and uncertainty over genuinely new sites. The published means cannot establish a unique best general-purpose model.
- **HTAN (Zhang et al., Wiley, 14 July 2026)** has a verified primary abstract claiming mean scan-wise Pearson **.82 ± .02**, individual **.80–.86**, and explicitly distinguishes ROI predictions from pseudo-volume visualization. Full methods, number of people/sites, split independence, aggregation and matched baselines remain **unverified**. Retain as a notable claim, not the winner. [Primary publisher abstract](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/cpe.70860).
- **EF-Diffusion**, Electronics 14(22):4432, **13 November 2025**, is a verified primary publication; full retrieval was blocked here. Its exact numbers, splits and input requirements are **unverified**. **NeuroCycle** was located as an anonymous ICLR-2026 submission PDF; acceptance and quantitative results were not independently established. Neither is ranked. [EF-Diffusion](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/14/22/4432); [NeuroCycle submission](https://openreview.net/pdf/198093530874d5151e666842aca4ea7d812be705.pdf).

## 2. Do site transfer, clinical evidence and inference inputs support scanner-free replacement?

### Takeaway

There is **positive but small healthy-cohort unseen-site reconstruction evidence**, and there is an important **cleared targeted clinical EEG intervention**. Those are different successes. The gathered literature does not establish broad, accurate diagnostic/presurgical reconstruction of a new patient's contemporaneous measured BOLD from scanner-free EEG. [NeuroBOLT Table 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.05341v2); [FDA Prism clearance](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpmn/pmn.cfm?ID=K222101).

### Cited Findings

- **Healthy unseen-site positive example:** NeuroBOLT trained on VU rest and tested zero-shot on a different-site auditory task reports **seven-target mean temporal R .380**, versus **.373** when trained on that task; Table 3. The external dataset comprises **10 people/16 scans**, with **four test scans**. Site, hardware and condition change together; this is not a large multicenter resting-state replication or an equivalence test. UnEBOLT's 64-ROI external task result is **.377 ± .023 zero-shot**, **.406 ± .083 fine-tuned**, versus **.379 ± .071 from scratch**; Table 2. [NeuroBOLT §§3.1,3.4/Table 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.05341v2); [UnEBOLT Table 2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v315/main/assets/li26g/li26g.pdf).
- **UniEFS extends site/context evaluation:** Fig.4H and Appendix D.6/Fig.8 report leave-one-dataset-out comparisons across VU rest, NIH rest and NIH task. This is more than simply showing two datasets in a paper; exact graph-only bar values are **not transcribed as numerical results here**. Appendix B states that nine people overlap NIH rest/task and are kept in the same split across conditions. Main-test accuracy must not be relabeled unseen-site accuracy because NIH scans augment training. [UniEFS full proceedings PDF, Appendices B,C.1,D.6](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v306/main/assets/li26gp/li26gp.pdf).
- **UniEFS clinical transfer is meaningful representation evidence, not measured patient BOLD recovery:** Parkinson cohort starts at 50 people (25/25), with six excluded, leaving **44**. Appendix D.5/Table 7 reports **subject-level five-fold logistic probing: PD accuracy .75, F1 .73, AUC .87**, versus LaBraM accuracy .73/AUC .86. Thus the full paper contains more than t-SNE pictures. Nevertheless the evaluation uses **EEG-only clinical data with no paired BOLD reference**; a disease probe cannot validate synthesized time courses or abnormal-region localization. Vigilance prompts were omitted for this clinical evaluation. [UniEFS Appendix D.5, Table 7](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v306/main/assets/li26gp/li26gp.pdf).
- **Prism / Amyg-EFP is an important positive clinical precedent:** FDA **K222101**, decision **17 March 2023**, is Class II EEG biofeedback for relaxation/stress reduction and **adjunctive PTSD symptom treatment under a healthcare professional**, alongside other interventions. The FDA summary explicitly excludes diagnostic conclusions/classification/clinical interpretation. Its prospective **single-arm, open-label, unblinded** study had **five sites** (four Israeli/one US), **79 full-analysis, 66 efficacy, 63 per-protocol patients**. At three months, **66.67% of the efficacy set** had a ≥6-point CAPS-5 reduction. This is a treatment endpoint, not temporal BOLD R. [FDA decision and clinical summary, PDF pp.3–8](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf22/K222101.pdf).
- The **Amyg-EFP clinical paper** describes fMRI-informed EEG neurofeedback and Prism; its journal issue is **Psychiatry Research 333, March 2024**, despite the DOI containing 2023. Do not count its report and the FDA supporting study as separate confirmations merely because they have different document dates. EEG delivery of a learned, target-specific feedback signal is an actual clinical translation pathway; it does not require claiming a diagnostic brain image. [Primary publisher article](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0165178123006613).

**Inference-input audit.** “Not specified” is not proof that an input is absent. Structural MRI used to define/register training or evaluation targets is distinguished from anatomy supplied to the deployed network. No generic atlas can supply an individual's structural anatomy.

### Inferences

- **Static FC cannot establish the right trajectory.** A common time permutation of every predicted ROI preserves their FC matrix while ruining alignment with the measured BOLD timeline. Likewise, good spectral similarity can survive phase errors. These are mathematical reasons to require temporal R separately; FC, fingerprinting and power-spectrum agreement remain useful additional evidence.
- **Spatial image quality cannot establish dynamic recovery.** Anatomical/background intensity and population spatial priors can contribute heavily to full-volume SSIM or spatial R. The necessary comparison is against a mean/template and EEG-independent model on centered fluctuations, alongside per-region/voxel temporal metrics. This is an audit requirement, not a finding that any particular paper's entire score is artifact.
- **Task decoding is evidence of useful information, not sufficient evidence of personal BOLD.** The model may preserve task/state structure without recovering spontaneous deviations from the task mean. CATD's patient classification, UniEFS's clinical probe and video decoding from reconstructed fMRI therefore support narrower claims than faithful BOLD reconstruction.
- **Physiological confounds remain an identification problem.** Shared vigilance, systemic variation, motion and residual scanner/cardio artifacts can be predictive without proving region-specific neural recovery. Conversely, global/arousal coupling can be genuinely useful. Removing global signal changes the target and cannot alone prove artifact or a biological ceiling. Require nuisance-only, EEG-only and combined controls, and report regional residual performance; do not presuppose that any one model is contaminated.
- **Appropriate positive conclusion:** EEG contains enough information to estimate some measured BOLD dynamics and can support narrow fMRI-informed interventions. The evidence does not support replacing individual functional imaging broadly. Prism prevents the incorrect blanket statement that there are no clinical EEG/fMRI-derived products or patient studies.

### Gaps

- No gathered paper establishes simultaneously: frozen model, genuinely unseen clinical people and sites, intended outside-scanner EEG, independent contemporaneous measured BOLD, and faithful regional/voxel recovery with matched simple and physiological controls.
- Asynchronous stroke measurements cannot answer instantaneous reconstruction accuracy. EEG-only Parkinson cohorts cannot answer it either. These are different reference-standard limitations, not evidence that patient inference is impossible.
- UniEFS graph-only transfer values, new-dataset token handling, detailed uncertainty over sites and causal vigilance availability require further code/method verification. No numeric bar estimates or assumption of “equivalent to within-site training” is supplied.

### Inferences

**Concise synthesis for the report writer:** There is real, moderate measured-BOLD prediction in healthy unseen people, including a small zero-shot different-site result. Newer whole-atlas models improve coverage and matched benchmarks, especially UniEFS and MARBLE. The largest-looking numbers frequently refer to spatial image similarity, personally calibrated reconstruction, BOLD-assisted interpolation, clinical classification or static connectivity. Those cannot establish faithful scanner-free recovery of a new person's complete BOLD signal. A narrow clinical translation pathway already exists through Prism/Amyg-EFP; broad diagnostic functional-MRI substitution was not validated by the gathered evidence.

**Do not overclaim:**

1. Do not turn six test scans into six independent people, scans into participants, or windows/vertices into independent clinical cases. UnEBOLT explicitly shows five people underlying its six test scans.
2. Do not label Herzog's .323 as unseen-person accuracy, Qu's .824 as temporal recovery, CATD's BOLD-assisted ~.99 as EEG-only recovery, or a classifier's percentage as fMRI reconstruction accuracy.
3. Do not equate .473 on six selected regions plus global BOLD with .434 across 64 ROIs or .367 across 512. Do not treat FC correlation as temporal R.
4. Do not claim causal live inference from offline artifact removal, centered filtering, long sequence encoders or epoch-derived vigilance without checking latency and future-data access.
5. Do not say there is no clinical evidence or no FDA-cleared product. Say the verified clearance concerns **adjunctive neurofeedback treatment**, not diagnostic imaging or faithful whole-brain recovery.
6. Do not describe accessible code as independent replication, claim anatomy-free native-space reconstruction from an atlas output, or silently omit target-person calibration or measured fMRI anchors.
7. Do not report a universal best achievable R, a universal participant requirement or a fundamental EEG ceiling from these small, heterogeneous cohorts.

These are interpretations of the verified evidence above, not additional empirical results.

### Gaps

**Internal project context supplied by the user — not peer reviewed, no public source URL, not independently audited in this pass:**

- VU: **22 people/29 resting scans**; full ridge **seven-target R .381** versus shared **24-s NeuroBOLT .336**, subject-grouped CV.
- Removing global signal changes **64-region mean R .343 → .144**. This is a target-decomposition sensitivity result; it does not prove that all retained prediction was artifact, nor establish residual neural specificity.
- Frozen VU ridge on **28 ds006040 people**: eyes-closed **R .227**, versus **.228 trained within site**; eyes-open **.140**. **No demonstrated equivalence** follows from similar point estimates.
- Arousal-monitor global-BOLD prototype: streaming replay **r .263**; **not a prospective live scanner trial**.
- No fundamental ceiling is inferred from the small VU learning curve. Exact internal averaging, uncertainty, preprocessing and deployment details are not independently reconstructed here. These values should not be mixed into a peer-reviewed leaderboard or presented as a matched replication of the original fixed split.

Other unresolved issues: patient-level amplitude/lag agreement and abnormality preservation; complete clinical code/data releases; uncertainty for a genuinely new site; preprocessing's dependence on scanner artifacts/auxiliary sensors; and how much regional recovery survives matched nuisance/global-component controls. These are the limits on the present conclusion, not findings of impossibility.
