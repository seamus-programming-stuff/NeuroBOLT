# Paired EEG–fMRI data, model scale and a small-team roadmap

## Accessible paired acquisition: interim verified inventory

### Takeaway
Interim evidence saved 21 October 2026 (Australia/Sydney); audit continues. Public paired releases provide tens of people per source and substantial within-person state variation, not a demonstrated thousand-person clinical training corpus. Recorded EEG time, nominal fMRI protocol time, synchronized overlap and usable post-QC paired time must be reported separately.

### Cited Findings
- **ds006040:** primary descriptor published 3 March 2026; 28 participants, one visit, 63 scalp channels plus ECG, 3 T Siemens Prisma. Ten fMRI runs/person: eyes-open and eyes-closed rest, checkerboard, three gradCPT, two imagery and two dual-task runs. Rest and cognitive blocks last 360 s; checkerboard is seven 50 s cycles. Nominal paired protocol arithmetic: 28 × (9 × 360 + 350) / 3,600 = **27.92 participant-hours**, including **5.60 resting hours**. These are protocol-derived hours before exclusions. Scanner-OFF EEG is additional unpaired data. The paper reports modality-specific exclusions and describes GSR in its analysis preprocessing. — [Peer-reviewed descriptor](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-026-06616-6); [raw/preprocessed file and naming documentation](https://github.com/OpenNeuroDatasets/ds006040/blob/70db5b19189043462a46772e814d70d252e8844d/README_dataset.txt); [CC0 metadata](https://github.com/OpenNeuroDatasets/ds006040/blob/70db5b19189043462a46772e814d70d252e8844d/dataset_description.json).
- **ds003768:** 33 healthy people at Penn State; two nominal 10-minute rest runs and multiple nominal 15-minute sleep runs/person. Direct repository inventory identifies 66 rest and 189 sleep BOLD run sidecars: **66 × 10/60 + 189 × 15/60 = 58.25 nominal paired hours**. Exact synchronized/post-QC hours are not established by that arithmetic. Raw BrainVision EEG, BOLD, and expert sleep-stage annotations are available; metadata identifies CC0. — [Peer-reviewed descriptor](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2023.109059); [acquisition README](https://github.com/OpenNeuroDatasets/ds003768/blob/a3b1ccbc491ab764618bda8611311eb710ebbe38/README); [pinned repository tree](https://github.com/OpenNeuroDatasets/ds003768/tree/a3b1ccbc491ab764618bda8611311eb710ebbe38); [license metadata](https://github.com/OpenNeuroDatasets/ds003768/blob/a3b1ccbc491ab764618bda8611311eb710ebbe38/dataset_description.json).

Protocol-derived paired time is **34 × 10/60 + 5 × 27/60 ≈ 7.92 hours**. Do not report 34 public learning-task participants. - **NATVIEW:** 22 healthy adults, two intended visits, scanner-ON paired acquisition plus scanner-OFF and outside-scanner EEG controls. Raw and processed data are publicly hosted through INDI; data/code CC BY 4.0. ### Inferences
- Summed protocol durations are acquisition inventories, not independent samples and not a harmonized training set. No pooled usable-hour claim should be made until per-run modality overlap, preprocessing, exclusions and duplicated derivative files are resolved.
- Sleep adds long within-person trajectories, but 175 recorded hours from 16 people does not supply the anatomical, age, disease or site diversity of 175 independent people.

### Gaps
- Complete NATVIEW run inventory; VU release license and available derivative scope; exact post-QC synchronized hours for every release remain to be finalized below.

## What is empirically known about scaling?

### Takeaway
There is no verified EEG-to-fMRI scaling law here establishing that hundreds or thousands of participants will achieve clinically useful accuracy. Closely related neuroimaging evidence supports measuring sample size, scan duration, reliability and site diversity jointly; it does not provide a transferable accuracy threshold.

### Cited Findings
- **Ooi et al., Nature 2025, peer-reviewed:** phenotype prediction from static fMRI functional connectivity, not EEG-to-BOLD time-course translation. Main HCP analysis: 792 retained people, subject/family-grouped nested CV; ABCD resting analysis: 2,565 retained people, 22 sites grouped into 10 site-clusters, with three clusters held out per outer split. Learning curves vary training people and minutes while keeping test sets fixed. Both data dimensions help; longer scans show diminishing returns, and the paper explicitly says it did not test cross-dataset prediction. — [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09250-1); [full-text primary article mirror](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12367542/).
- **Gell et al., Nature Communications 2024, peer-reviewed:** measurement noise/reliability analysis, including learning curves in 5,000 UK Biobank participants. Only sufficiently reliable measures fully benefit from enlarging samples from hundreds to thousands; this concerns brain–behaviour prediction, not paired EEG-fMRI translation. — [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-54022-6).
- **Ye et al., 23 September 2026, preprint:** controlled fMRI-only foundation-model scaling, six nested recording-count subsets and encoders approximately 2.65–92.59 million parameters; approximately 10,347 GPU-hours for the entire study. At matched compute, adding data benefits more tasks than adding parameters. Participant partitions are 60:20:20; downstream validation/test people are excluded from pretraining. Final OOD model selection excludes entire OOD datasets, whereas descriptive scaling analyses also inspect OOD task scores. No paired EEG or EEG-to-fMRI endpoint. — [Preprint v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.27232v1).

### Inferences
- Use hundreds as a staged empirical learning-curve experiment, and thousands only as a conditional expansion if external-site and clinically relevant outcomes continue improving. Neither number is a measured clinical requirement.
- Separate time-course Pearson r within a scan from correlation across people for a phenotype, static FC reconstruction, task activation maps, and masked fMRI reconstruction; these quantities do not share a scaling curve.

### Gaps
- A sufficiently powered, directly relevant, cross-person and cross-site paired EEG-fMRI data/model-size scaling experiment has not been verified in this audit.

## Hypothetical resource ranges and go/no-go roadmap

### Takeaway
Planning proposals below are explicitly hypothetical. The completed audit will add model-size brackets, transparent paired-hour arithmetic, storage/compute estimates and milestones, without treating participant count as a guarantee.

### Cited Findings
- Monash Biomedical Imaging publicly lists human 3 T MRI scanner rates of AUD 660/h for Biograph and AUD 850/h for CIMA X in its 2025 rate column, with stated annual-increase rules. These are scanner service anchors, not combined EEG-fMRI study quotes. — [Official prices](https://www.monash.edu/researchinfrastructure/mbi/forms-and-policies/prices).

### Inferences
- An illustrative 1,000-person programme with two visits × 45 paired minutes yields **1,500 acquired participant-hours**. At an assumed 75% retention, that is **1,125 usable paired hours**. It is not 1,000 independent hours or proof of clinical readiness. Booking time, preparation, screening, missing visits and staffing must be budgeted separately.
- First discriminating experiment: crossed learning curves for training participants × minutes/person × EEG channels/added physiology, using fixed participant/site holdouts, nested tuning and repeat visits. Compare ridge and a compact smooth spectral/spatial/HRF model before expanding parameter counts. Report global-signal-retained, GSR and physiology-controlled targets, and lock the intended clinical endpoint independently of generic reconstruction r.

### Gaps
- All-in acquisition quotes, actual preprocessing throughput, memory/storage profiles and meaningful endpoint-specific decision margins require a local pilot; they cannot be recovered from participant counts or GPU marketing specifications.
