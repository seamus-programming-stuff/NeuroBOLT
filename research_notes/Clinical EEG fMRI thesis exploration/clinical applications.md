## 1. Which evidence actually supports each proposed clinical use?

### Takeaway

The clearest clinical translation is **targeted fMRI-informed EEG neurofeedback**, exemplified by Prism's narrow PTSD indication. UnEBOLT and MARBLE establish healthy technical prediction; UniEFS additionally supplies patient EEG representation evidence, not measured patient BOLD recovery. [FDA indications](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf22/K222101.pdf); [UnEBOLT](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v315/li26g.html); [MARBLE](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026/0612-Paper4437.html); [UniEFS](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/li26gp.html).

### Cited Findings

**Poster-ready evidence-to-use-case matrix.** C = demonstrated clinical use/clearance for the specified intervention; P = patient proof of concept or patient study, not clearance; H = healthy technical validation; F = proposed use without the required application validation. Categories apply to the named evidence, not automatically to every model in the row.

| Use case | Best direct evidence / level | What the three requested models support | Defensible poster claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| PTSD neurofeedback | **C:** Prism/Amyg-EFP; adjunctive PTSD biofeedback under professional supervision. [FDA](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf22/K222101.pdf) | No treatment trial in the three model papers; their regional outputs offer candidate feedback signals only. | Targeted EEG feedback informed by fMRI has reached clinical use; new whole-atlas models need their own intervention validation. |
| Arousal / scan support | **H → F:** UniEFS uses vigilance as input; alerting/workflow benefit untested. [Methods, Appendix B](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v306/main/assets/li26gp/li26gp.pdf) | UniEFS supports state-conditioned reconstruction; UnEBOLT/MARBLE supply regional prediction, not validated wakefulness alerts. | Plausible advisory application; test added value over direct EEG vigilance scoring. |

[Appendix D.5/Table 7](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v306/main/assets/li26gp/li26gp.pdf) | Evidence belongs to **UniEFS**, not UnEBOLT or MARBLE. | An fMRI-informed EEG representation carries disease information; patient fMRI fidelity remains untested. |
| Stroke rehabilitation feedback | **P:** randomized real EEG–fMRI/EEG neurofeedback programme. [Butet 2025](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12984-025-01598-9) | None of the three models was used in that trial. [Koupparis 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34400584/) | Healthy atlas prediction and a PD probe do not establish epileptogenic localization, language lateralization or eloquent-cortex mapping. | EEG-only diagnostic fMRI substitution remains unestablished by this evidence. |

**Clinical additions beyond the earlier report:**

- **Clearance anchor:** FDA K222101, decision **17 March 2023**, authorizes adjunctive PTSD symptom biofeedback, together with other interventions. The summary excludes diagnostic conclusions/classification/clinical interpretation. Its five-site, open-label single-arm study included **79 full-analysis / 66 efficacy / 63 per-protocol** participants; **66.67% of the efficacy set** achieved a ≥6-point CAPS-5 reduction at three months. This does not measure BOLD reconstruction accuracy. [FDA decision record](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpmn/pmn.cfm?ID=K222101); [summary, PDF pp. 3–8](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf22/K222101.pdf).
- **Controlled patient target-engagement precedent:** the 2021 Amyg-EFP study randomized **59 adults** to trauma-script feedback, neutral feedback or no neurofeedback, with **15 sessions** and actual fMRI neurofeedback assessments of transfer. It lacked a sham-feedback arm; exposure and nonspecific contributions remained unresolved. This is a useful precedent for testing a specified surrogate, not whole-brain replacement. [Fruchtman-Steinbok et al., full article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8551212/).
- **New community-use evidence, March 2026:** a retrospective Prism series had **28 consecutive patients, 21 completers**, mean PCL-5 reduction **37.0 ± 18.2 points**, and follow-up for only three. Table 3 distinguishes **19/21 (90.5%)** with ≥10-point improvement from **18/21 (85.7%)** with ≥15-point improvement; the discussion/conclusion call the latter “clinically significant” without preserving this threshold distinction. Use the table's explicit thresholds. No control, 25% discontinuation and limited follow-up prevent causal or durable-efficacy conclusions. Its references to the Fruchter study as controlled also conflict with that study's FDA-described single-arm design. [Ghelber et al., original article](https://doi.org/10.3390/jcm15062122); [full text/Table 3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13026192/).
- **Current trial status matters:** **NCT06770998**, last update posted **1 April 2026**, is recruiting, estimated **250 participants**, randomized and double-masked to participants/assessors, Prism versus sham. Primary outcome is CAPS-5-R change at Week 9; estimated primary completion **June 2027**; **no results posted** in the primary API record at this audit. It is prospective evidence in progress, not an established 2026 result. [Registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06770998); [primary structured record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT06770998).
- **A completed RCT is not necessarily a sham efficacy test:** DRKS00031532 reports **138 participants**, completion **28 August 2025**, comparing **15 sessions plus two boosters versus eight sessions**, both active Amyg-EFP regimens. The registry's publication/results fields were empty when checked. Completion cannot establish benefit versus sham, and missing registry results do not prove no publication exists elsewhere. [Primary German registry](https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00031532).
- **High-value rehabilitation addition:** Butet et al. randomized **32** chronic stroke patients; **30 completed**, 15 per arm. Over five weeks, neurofeedback combined **five real bimodal EEG-fMRI sessions plus nine EEG-only sessions**; control received **14 motor-imagery sessions without feedback**. FMA-UE change differed between groups (**p=.048**); ≥4-point responders were **8/15 versus 3/15**. M1/SMA activation-change comparisons were nonsignificant. This single-centre, assessor-blinded study in selected patients with partly preserved corticospinal tracts supports the combined programme, not the isolated contribution of synthesized BOLD, EEG alone, or any requested model. [Butet et al. 2025, methods/results](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12984-025-01598-9).
- **Patient synthesis evidence requires a different qualifier:** Peng et al. included **13 stroke patients and 14 controls**, with EEG and resting fMRI acquired on **different days**. The stated subject split is **9/2/2**; results use two stroke and two control test cases. Selected modified-model stroke correlations include central sulcus **.62 ± .09** and superior precentral gyrus **.72 ± .06**. These are two ROI examples, not a whole-brain mean. Different-day spontaneous traces cannot establish recovery of the BOLD trajectory contemporaneous with the EEG; FM-UE characterized eligibility, not validated recovery prediction. The control split accounting for all 14 people is unclear. [Full manuscript, §§II–III/Table II](https://publish.illinois.edu/ritahtpeng/files/2026/01/IEEE2025_revised__Asynchronous_EEG_to_fMRI_Translation_Using_Multi_dimensional_Feature_Mapping_in_Stroke-3.pdf); [IEEE NER 2025 programme](https://cmsworkshops.com/NER2025/view_paper.php?PaperNum=1300).
- **Mapping comparator:** actual EEG-fMRI was related to **106 surgeries in 84 epilepsy patients**, with ≥1-year follow-up. Failure to resect the principal response was associated with poor outcomes, while removing it did not ensure seizure freedom. This provides a patient outcome reference for localization research, not evidence that an EEG-only generator reproduces those maps. [Koupparis et al., original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34400584/).

### Inferences

- The most useful new distinction is **intervention benefit versus signal fidelity**. A feedback intervention can have a clinical effect without supplying a diagnostic image; conversely, BOLD correlation alone cannot establish therapeutic benefit. The FDA intervention and Butet trial support testing a narrowly defined feedback system, not transferring their outcomes to a different model. [FDA](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf22/K222101.pdf); [Butet](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12984-025-01598-9).
- Arousal support should demonstrate why synthesizing BOLD helps when EEG is already available. Goodale et al. showed **fMRI-to-alertness** estimation and behavioural associations in healthy participants: useful physiological rationale, but the reverse direction from EEG-to-fMRI and not a trial of a scanner advisory. [Goodale et al. 2021](https://elifesciences.org/articles/62376).

### Gaps

- No model-specific clearance, prospective clinical utility trial or validated EEG-only diagnostic replacement was located for the three named models in the bounded proceedings/publisher, FDA-indexed and trial-indexed search. This is not an exhaustive worldwide regulatory search. Prism's Australian ARTG status was not established; FDA clearance cannot be presented as Australian authorization. [FDA scope actually verified](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf22/K222101.pdf); [Australian evidence requirements](https://www.tga.gov.au/products/medical-devices/software-and-artificial-intelligence-ai/manufacturing/artificial-intelligence-ai-and-medical-device-software-regulation/evidence-requirements-software-using-ai).

## 2. What exactly do UnEBOLT, UniEFS and MARBLE demonstrate?

### Takeaway

**“Unibolt” should be written UnEBOLT. It is not UniEFS.** UnEBOLT uses ROI-conditioned adapters/heads; UniEFS adds an fMRI-pretrained decoder and context prompts; MARBLE combines Mamba/attention with ROI cross-attention. Their output is atlas-region time courses. [UnEBOLT record](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v315/li26g.html); [UniEFS author overview](https://soupeeli.github.io/UniEFS/); [MARBLE record](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026/0612-Paper4437.html).

### Cited Findings

**Exact benchmark ledger.** Temporal r is Pearson correlation with measured BOLD over time. FC correlation compares connectivity-matrix edges. ± denotes reported SD, not confidence intervals; scans from one person are not independent people.

| Model / publication | Cohort, split and inputs | Reported results and practical limit |
|---|---|---|
| **UnEBOLT — MIDL 2026** | VU **22 healthy people / 29 scans**, one site; **18/5/6 train/validation/test scans**, subject-disjoint; Table 3 identifies **five test people**, one contributing two scans. **26 channels, 200 Hz, preceding 16 s**. | **64 ROIs:** temporal **r=.418±.036**, matched NeuroBOLT **.413±.052**; FC **r=.549±.120**. At **128/256 ROIs:** temporal **.386±.044/.360±.047**. Separate auditory cohort **10 people/16 scans**, **9/3/4** split, 23 common channels: zero-shot **.377±.023**, fine-tuned **.406±.083**, scratch **.379±.071**. Healthy transfer, not patient validation. [Tables 1–3, §§3.1/3.2.3](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v315/main/assets/li26g/li26g.pdf) |

FC **.456±.129**, versus **.469** for both comparators; top-25%-edge FC-F1 **.454±.024**. **2.6M** parameters versus UnEBOLT **8.2M**; reported peak memory **6.0 versus 13.8 GB**. - **UniEFS's clinical result is a different endpoint:** **44 EEG-only people, 22 PD/22 controls**, after six exclusions; **subject-level five-fold logistic probing** yields accuracy **.75**, F1 **.73**, AUC **.87**; LaBraM accuracy **.73**, AUC **.86**. The representation is frozen/zero-shot, but the classifier is fitted with disease labels. No paired patient BOLD. [Appendix D.5/Table 7](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v306/main/assets/li26gp/li26gp.pdf).
- **Arousal qualification:** UniEFS's vigilance prompt is derived using **63-s epochs**; it is an input, not an independently validated vigilance prediction. It was omitted from the PD evaluation. A causal, low-latency clinical implementation is unestablished. [Appendices B/D.5](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v306/main/assets/li26gp/li26gp.pdf).
- **Access remains a translation constraint:** UniEFS's author page says code/data **coming soon**. MARBLE's paper promises weights, but its README lists checkpoint release as pending and shows example training settings different from paper defaults. Freeze the actual release/configuration before claiming reproducibility; neither was rerun here. [UniEFS author page](https://soupeeli.github.io/UniEFS/); [MARBLE repository](https://github.com/emeelkaa/marble).

### Inferences

- Compare models only within matched protocols: **.434 across 64 ROIs** and **.367 across 512** are not one leaderboard. MARBLE's own comparison shows a temporal/efficiency improvement alongside lower FC correlation. FC similarity does not certify the correct timeline: applying a common time permutation to all ROI traces preserves their FC. This is a mathematical observation, not an empirical accusation. [MARBLE Table 1](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026/paper/4437_paper.pdf); [UniEFS author results](https://soupeeli.github.io/UniEFS/).
- The three main benchmark results reuse the VU cohort; they are not three independent clinical replications. UniEFS adds NIH paired training and HCP pretraining, as specified in the ledger. [UnEBOLT dataset](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v315/main/assets/li26g/li26g.pdf); [UniEFS dataset](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v306/main/assets/li26gp/li26gp.pdf); [MARBLE dataset](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026/paper/4437_paper.pdf).
- A Parkinson probe demonstrates useful information in EEG embeddings. It cannot establish accurate BOLD dynamics, localize Parkinson pathology or show that synthesized BOLD adds diagnostic value beyond direct EEG. The paper's own representation framing is the appropriate claim. [UniEFS Appendix D.5](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v306/main/assets/li26gp/li26gp.pdf).

### Gaps

- Native patient voxel maps, out-of-scanner regional fidelity, repeat-day patient performance, prospective streaming performance and patient decision benefit remain unestablished by these benchmarks. The reported tests do not provide clinical sensitivity/specificity. [UnEBOLT methods](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v315/main/assets/li26g/li26g.pdf); [MARBLE limitations](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026/paper/4437_paper.pdf); [UniEFS author scope](https://soupeeli.github.io/UniEFS/).
- UniEFS Table 7 also lists **AD AUC=1.00 for several encoders**; that row lacks sufficient cohort/fold detail in the inspected clinical section for a credible diagnostic claim, and has no paired BOLD reference. It is deliberately excluded from the poster. Some appendix cortical/subcortical values differ from the main Table 1; the ledger uses main-table values consistently. [Full appendix](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v306/main/assets/li26gp/li26gp.pdf).

## 3. What clinical gate would make a follow-up defensible?

### Takeaway

The realistic first gate is a **single, prespecified clinical action**, not general replacement of fMRI. Current TGA guidance requires independent data, fit-for-purpose references, representative intended populations, workflow evaluation and external validation proportionate to risk; it does not provide a universal acceptable BOLD r. [TGA evidence requirements, checked 21 October 2026](https://www.tga.gov.au/products/medical-devices/software-and-artificial-intelligence-ai/manufacturing/artificial-intelligence-ai-and-medical-device-software-regulation/evidence-requirements-software-using-ai).

### Cited Findings

- The verified TGA page, updated **4 September 2025**, explicitly considers dependence across patients/sites/acquisition, Australian clinical applicability, statistically justified dataset sizes, human–AI use and confounding. These are evidence principles, not clearance of any named model or prescribed numerical acceptance criteria. [Primary guidance](https://www.tga.gov.au/products/medical-devices/software-and-artificial-intelligence-ai/manufacturing/artificial-intelligence-ai-and-medical-device-software-regulation/evidence-requirements-software-using-ai).

### Inferences

**Proposed application gates below are recommendations, not completed studies or regulator-set thresholds.** They translate the intended-use principles above into testable decisions. [TGA basis](https://www.tga.gov.au/products/medical-devices/software-and-artificial-intelligence-ai/manufacturing/artificial-intelligence-ai-and-medical-device-software-regulation/evidence-requirements-software-using-ai).

| Proposed application | Minimum meaningful next evidence / stop rule |
|---|---|
| **Arousal advisory: first candidate for a narrow workflow study** | Freeze a fully causal pipeline; compare direct EEG vigilance, simple EEG-to-BOLD/ridge, physiology and the proposed model on independent visits/sites. Reference sustained loss of wakefulness with blinded scoring plus corroborating eye/behavioural measures where suitable. Prespecify episode sensitivity, false alarms/hour, onset delay, calibrated probabilities and monitoring coverage. Begin with silent prospective evaluation; a workflow claim additionally needs fewer unusable/repeated acquisitions without excessive interruption. If synthesized BOLD adds no useful information beyond EEG, simplify the product claim. |
| **One ROI/network neurofeedback signal** | Establish target engagement against actual paired patient fMRI, including response direction, amplitude, lag, repeatability and artifact/respiration controls. Then test the new feedback intervention against an appropriate credible comparator with blinded symptom/function assessment, durability and adverse effects. Prism's indication/evidence does not transfer to a new generator or indication. |
| **PD or other disease representation** | Use an external patient-level cohort, including relevant differential diagnoses, and compare against direct EEG/foundation embeddings and demographic/site/medication baselines. Prespecify discrimination, calibration and decision benefit. A claim of recovered disease BOLD separately requires actual paired patient fMRI; classifier performance cannot meet that gate. |
| **Stroke rehabilitation / longitudinal monitoring** | Separate baseline impairment, future recovery and treatment-response targets. Test patient-disjoint and visit-disjoint performance against measured network features and change in FMA-UE/ARAT, controlling baseline severity and treatment. Different-day recordings may assess stable network properties; they cannot reference the exact ongoing trajectory. A treatment claim needs a controlled intervention study of the specific feedback system. |
| **Diagnostic/presurgical mapping** | Require prospective intended-population mapping validation, clinically meaningful localization/lateralization errors, uncertainty/abstention, appropriate invasive/stimulation evidence where available, and outcomes. Include abnormal anatomy and difficult cases; retain needed anatomical imaging. Healthy ROI r, FC, image resemblance and PD AUC cannot clear this gate. |

**Poster sentence to keep:** “EEG-to-fMRI models predict part of healthy regional BOLD and may support narrow clinical tools. Clinical translation is established for a specific fMRI-informed EEG neurofeedback intervention; faithful EEG-only diagnostic fMRI recovery in new patients remains unestablished.” This synthesis is bounded to the verified sources and applications above. ### Gaps

- There is no justified universal correlation threshold, cohort size or calendar date for clinical readiness in the inspected evidence. Acceptance margins and independent patient/event counts must follow the intended action and error consequences. These notes propose gates; they do not certify that a model meets them. [TGA intended-purpose and dataset-size requirements](https://www.tga.gov.au/products/medical-devices/software-and-artificial-intelligence-ai/manufacturing/artificial-intelligence-ai-and-medical-device-software-regulation/evidence-requirements-software-using-ai).
