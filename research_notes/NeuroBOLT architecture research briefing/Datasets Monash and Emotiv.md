## 1. Which paired EEG–fMRI datasets can support a realistic NeuroBOLT extension?

### Takeaway

Use the existing **VU or ds006040 paired data** for the two-week core, with one bounded architecture/measurement ablation. **MULTI-CLARID** is the strongest next public candidate for an eyes-closed, different-scanner test; **ds007216** is attractive for repeat-session and task transfer. NATVIEW has already been investigated here, and ATR requires permission. These are planning judgments based on the release contracts and project history below, not new experimental results. [VU release](https://huggingface.co/datasets/NeurdyLab/NeuroBOLT/blob/main/README.md), [ds006040](https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds006040/versions/1.0.2), [MULTI-CLARID](https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds005795/versions/1.0.0), [ds007216](https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds007216/versions/1.1.0), [completed NATVIEW record](<C:/Users/Seamus/Documents/GitHub/NeuroBOLT/research_notes/Clinical EEG fMRI thesis exploration/NATVIEW experiments.md>), [ATR access](https://bicr.atr.jp/dbi/download/dataset-of-eeg-fmri-with-cwl/).

### Inferences

#### Proposed two-week experiment, with a bounded extension

**Academic question:** “How much does electrode coverage affect a NeuroBOLT-style model's prediction of measured ROI BOLD in unseen participants?” An ablation is a deliberate removal or change of one component while keeping the comparison conditions fixed. This connects model architecture to information available at its input, with an outcome that can be negative and still informative. The proposed question is motivated by the released 26-channel input and known montage problems, not by an assumed Emotiv dataset. [NeuroBOLT release](https://huggingface.co/datasets/NeurdyLab/NeuroBOLT/blob/main/README.md), [observed montage bridge problems](<C:/Users/Seamus/Documents/GitHub/NeuroBOLT/research_notes/Clinical EEG fMRI thesis exploration/NATVIEW experiments.md>).

| Proposed period | Concrete deliverable | Boundary for two-week feasibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–2 | Select one existing paired cohort, one/few ROI targets and a participant-separated split; diagram EEG history → encoder/features → BOLD prediction. Document units, channels, reference, timestamps and target preprocessing. | Reuse the existing loader/cache and completed results. Identify development exposure rather than claiming an untouched test. |
| Days 3–5 | Reproduce a simple lagged spectral ridge baseline and a small, specified NeuroBOLT comparison or reproduce existing predictions/figures if full training is impractical. | Do not promise training several newer architectures or reproducing every paper. |
| Days 6–8 | Perform **one primary ablation**: full available montage versus a physically documented reduced montage, using identical people, target frames and training budget. | Only measured overlapping sites count as measurements. Synthetic channel removal tests information loss, not real headset equivalence. |
| Days 9–10 | Evaluate per-person correlation, predictive R², amplitude ratio and retained-frame coverage; include a mean/template baseline and a temporal-shift negative control. | Keep all runs of each person together. Fit scalers and tune choices on training/validation only. |
| Days 11–14 | Build explanatory architecture figures, results and limitations; prepare the research-directions presentation/PDF. | Optional external-site result only if the adapter passes QC in time; otherwise report its exact unresolved requirements. |

This schedule is a **proposal**, not a measured runtime or assurance of completion. Its scope uses existing pipelines and evaluations documented in the project reports. [Existing hypothesis study](<C:/Users/Seamus/Documents/GitHub/NeuroBOLT/reports/NeuroBOLT hypothesis tests.md>), [later comparison and limitations](<C:/Users/Seamus/Documents/GitHub/NeuroBOLT/reports/Clinical EEG fMRI thesis exploration.md>).

- **Next public external candidate:** reserve MULTI-CLARID for a separately specified site transfer test once reference, BOLD preprocessing and atlas extraction are established. Its Philips acquisition and EC rest make the contrast useful, but “site” remains entangled with hardware and cleaning. ds007216 is the alternative if repeat-session/task reliability is the primary question. [MULTI-CLARID contract](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenNeuroDatasets/ds005795/master/README), [ds007216 design](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenNeuroDatasets/ds007216/master/README).
- **Representation learning is a secondary direction:** compare a frozen/pretrained EEG encoder with the same encoder initialized without pretraining, using the same paired BOLD labels and budget. More EEG-only recordings may help learn signal structure, but cannot create observed BOLD labels. BENDR provides a published EEG self-supervision precedent; a benefit for NeuroBOLT remains a hypothesis. [BENDR original paper](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2021.653659), [NeuroBOLT paired target design](https://github.com/soupeeli/NeuroBOLT).
- **Meaningful applications at this stage:** a reproducible cross-site EEG–BOLD benchmark, a tested montage/quality sensitivity result, or evidence that paired supervision improves an EEG representation. BOLD-derived representations for later behavioural prediction are a further testable application, not a demonstrated diagnostic product. The presence of attention responses in ds006040/ds007216 offers measured secondary endpoints if their timing is validated. [ds006040](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-026-06616-6), [ds007216](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.02.04.703882).

### Inferences

#### Testable research and business value hypotheses

These are **proposed benefits to test**, with no claim of revenue, customer demand, clinical effectiveness or MRI replacement.

| Hypothesis | Academic test and concrete output | Possible value if supported |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Quality-aware learning retains more useful data.** | Compare quality-informed masking/weighting with amplitude rejection and available CQ/EQ indicators; report downstream error **at matched retained coverage**, plus robustness under genuine artifacts. Use real quality annotations where available. | A reproducible quality benchmark or pipeline recommendation for research users; possible reduction in unusable recordings remains unmeasured. |
| **A representation transfers better between headsets/sessions.** | Use the portable comparison dataset with person-separated evaluation; compare spectral features, a small learned encoder and one pretraining variant under identical labels. Keep device/session boundaries explicit. | Better documented portability and fewer unsupported cross-device claims; no current product improvement demonstrated. |
| **Fewer channels preserve a specified BOLD target.** | On paired public data, compare real measured channel subsets at equal training budget, then test actual Emotiv EEG separately if access later exists. Report the error–channel-count curve and missing-site limitations. | Evidence to guide task-specific sensor configurations and measurement tradeoffs; no new hardware recommendation before actual validation. |
| **Paired BOLD supervision improves an EEG representation.** | Fit the EEG-to-BOLD task on authorized paired data; freeze the encoder and test a separate behavioural endpoint against EEG-only pretraining. BOLD prediction and behavioural transfer need separate results. | A well-specified multimodal representation benchmark; benefit is falsifiable and not guaranteed. |

The hypotheses are grounded in the paired-data differences, BENDR's self-supervised precedent, actual cross-device comparison data and existing vendor quality tools. Their outcome is unknown. **Purpose statement:** “We are scoping a short academic study of EEG representations, channel coverage and prediction of measured regional BOLD. Public paired EEG–fMRI data are the core. 2. **Actual cohort and labels:** unique participants, sessions/repeats, minutes/run, tasks, demographics where releasable, measured behavioural outcomes and label provenance. 4. **Pairing:** any actual **simultaneous** measured BOLD, scanner/site/TR, run-level pairing keys, triggers, initial-volume removal, clock drift/excisions and preprocessing/atlas definitions. If there is no simultaneous BOLD, record that explicitly and limit the use to EEG pretraining or robustness.
5. **Permissions:** authorized research purpose and named users, data agreement/consent constraints, institutional ethics route, approved storage/compute, ability to publish aggregate results/code, restrictions on examples, redistribution and trained-weight release.
