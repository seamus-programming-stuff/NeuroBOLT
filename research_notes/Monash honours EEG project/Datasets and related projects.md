# Dataset and project options for an introductory Monash CS AI honours project

As of **26 October 2026**. Focused recommendation after comparing the existing paired ds006040 route with a public Monash-associated EEG starter. This audit read primary dataset pages, metadata, papers and original code pages. No raw recordings were downloaded and no modelling pipeline was run. Recommendations below are proposed project designs, not experimental results.

## 1. Which public dataset route should be the core, and which paired extension is realistic?

### Takeaway

**For an introductory honours project, choose one public EEG subset of the Monash-associated DREAM collection as the core; use the existing ds006040 paired EEG–fMRI data as an optional extension.** Choose ds006040 as the core instead if predicting measured BOLD is an essential thesis question and a supervisor can provide an already checked alignment/preprocessing pipeline. Sleep-EDF is the simpler EEG fallback if the DREAM subset proves awkward. These are feasibility judgments based on the labels and released modalities below. [DREAM subset](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Zhang_Wamsley_2019_Final/22226692); [ds006040 data paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13066628/); [Sleep-EDF](https://physionet.org/content/sleep-edfx/1.0.0/sleep-telemetry/)

### Cited Findings

#### Four concrete options

[ds006040 technical validation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13066628/)

#### DREAM count and access audit

- The **published initial release** reports **20 datasets, 505 participants and 2,643 awakenings**. It contains M/EEG and dream-report data; it is not an EEG–fMRI dataset. Packages may be open or available by request. [Original DREAM paper, including data availability](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12350935/)
- **Catalogue v9**, published 30 October 2025 and inspected for this audit, contains 23 dataset-table rows including amendments. Filtering `Latest amendment = TRUE` and `Revoked = FALSE` leaves **20 active datasets: 19 marked Open and one Private**. Joining the record table on active `(Set ID, Amendment)` gives **2,669 cases and 508 distinct within-dataset participant IDs**. This is an audit calculation, **not proof of 508 unique people across studies**. The open packages include a MEG entry, so “19 open datasets” must not be rewritten as “19 EEG datasets.” [Versioned catalogue](https://bridges.monash.edu/articles/dataset/The_DREAM_database/22133105/9); [version/file metadata](https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles/22133105); [Datasets.csv](https://ndownloader.figshare.com/files/59170373); [Data records.csv](https://ndownloader.figshare.com/files/59170370)
- The active private entry is **SCANDataset: 18 participants, 85 cases**. Its restriction note requires an access request and says the contributor aims to provide a private link within two weeks. This is **request-controlled access**, not immediate public download. The catalogue's **CC BY 4.0 metadata licence must not be assumed to license every underlying recording package**; the proposed Zhang & Wamsley package separately declares CC BY 4.0. [Catalogue metadata and files](https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles/22133105); [selected package licence](https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles/22226692)
- Monash's news article gives **561 participants and 2,649 awakenings**, conflicting with the published paper and the current catalogue calculation. Do not silently substitute those publicity figures. Cite the paper for initial-release counts and the versioned catalogue for the current audit. [Monash news](https://www.monash.edu/medicine/news/latest/2025-articles/dream-start-for-database-that-aims-to-improve-our-understanding-of-conscious-experience-in-sleep); [original paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12350935/)

### Inferences

- **Why DREAM first:** one verified open subset gives a genuinely Monash-associated research connection, measured EEG and explicit labels without introducing MRI processing. Its 28-person size favours a modest feature-based study. “Monash-associated” describes the collection and research network; it does not mean these recordings were collected locally. [Subset and provenance](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Zhang_Wamsley_2019_Final/22226692); [Monash association in the paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12350935/)
- **Why ds006040 may instead be the core:** existing paired data remove a major acquisition/access dependency. However, the supplied EEG/behaviour files still need alignment checks, and BOLD analysis needs an explicit ROI definition and confound policy. The official pipeline spans MATLAB/EEGLAB, fMRIPrep and other MRI tools. Existing possession alone does not establish a beginner-ready loader. [Original preprocessing code](https://github.com/MoonBrainLab/GradCPT-Simultaneous-EEG-fMRI-DTI-Data); [data organization](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13066628/)
- **The extension is a separate supervised task:** DREAM or Sleep-EDF EEG cannot train EEG→fMRI without paired BOLD targets. EEG pretraining could transfer an encoder, but the EEG-to-BOLD mapping must be fitted/evaluated using paired ds006040 or VU examples. DWI, ordinary MRI or unrelated participants' fMRI do not supply time-aligned BOLD targets. [DREAM modalities](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12350935/); [paired ds006040 design](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13066628/); [NeuroBOLT training inputs](https://github.com/soupeeli/NeuroBOLT)

### Gaps

- Recording contents, missing channels, usable durations and actual post-QC class counts were not inspected. Public access/licensing was verified from primary pages and metadata; successful recording ingestion was not tested.
- The DREAM subset did not separately elicit “experience without recall.” Its binary report categories should not be treated as definitive presence/absence of consciousness or decoded dream content. [Subset label definitions](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Zhang_Wamsley_2019_Final/22226692)
- Existing local ds006040 processing, participant overlap with previous experiments and which outcomes have already influenced model choices were not audited. Do not describe it as an untouched external test without that audit.
- NATVIEW is a valid public paired reserve, but is excluded from the four-option shortlist to keep the project focused. It has 22 participants, two visits, eyes-open rest/movies, raw/preprocessed data under CC BY 4.0 and Schaefer-400 ROI outputs. Using it for the VU pipeline would require handling the atlas and condition differences. [Official release](https://fcon_1000.projects.nitrc.org/indi/retro/nat_view.html); [original descriptor](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-023-02458-8); [original code](https://github.com/NathanKlineInstitute/NATVIEW_EEGFMRI)

### Inferences

- A useful supervision arrangement would combine a CS/AI supervisor with a sleep/EEG domain adviser while preserving a public-data core that can proceed independently of a clinical agreement. DREAM supplies a documented connection for that discussion. [DREAM affiliations and contributors](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12350935/)
- Treat a clinical extension as a later, separately scoped study once the data custodian confirms permitted use, labels, suitable sample sizes and access arrangements. ### Gaps

- Current supervision capacity, CS honours eligibility, recording availability, participant counts, label completeness, approval lead times and project-specific licences for the local sleep/clinical opportunities are unconfirmed. No researchers were contacted.
- None of the verified Monash clinical pages establishes a public, simultaneously acquired EEG–BOLD dataset. Their existence should not be used to promise a local EEG→fMRI training cohort.

## 3. What should a beginner actually build, and how can it grow into a team?

### Takeaway

**Core proposal: “How well do simple EEG spectral features predict reported sleep experience in unseen participants?”** Use the single public DREAM subset, a majority-class baseline and logistic regression; add one small comparator or one channel-reduction experiment. The paired extension asks a different question: whether lagged EEG features predict measured ROI BOLD in ds006040. These are proposed scopes grounded in the released labels and original reproducible examples. [DREAM example analysis](https://zenodo.org/records/15234846); [ds006040 code](https://github.com/MoonBrainLab/GradCPT-Simultaneous-EEG-fMRI-DTI-Data)

### Cited Findings

#### Direct EEG prediction versus BOLD modelling

| Route | Supervision and output | Additional work | Beginner assessment — proposed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **DREAM EEG classification** | EEG → participant's report category. [Subset labels](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Zhang_Wamsley_2019_Final/22226692) | Channel/reference audit, artifact policy, fixed pre-awakening window, participant-grouped evaluation. | Best Monash-associated starter; keep the question narrow and model small. |
| **Sleep-EDF EEG classification** | EEG → technician-scored sleep stage. MNE supplies a reproducible example with 30-second epochs and five classes. [Official tutorial](https://mne.tools/stable/auto_tutorials/clinical/60_sleep.html) | Label mapping, class imbalance and keeping repeat nights together. | Strong fallback with a particularly clear tutorial path. |
| **Direct EEG regression in ds006040** | EEG → an independently measured continuous behavioural target, such as reaction time on relevant response trials. Response files are released. [Descriptor](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13066628/) | Event alignment, target construction and missing/incorrect response handling. | Feasible alternative if attention interests the student; not automatically easier than sleep classification. |
| **Paired EEG→BOLD regression** | EEG history → measured BOLD ROI series. NeuroBOLT requires synchronized EEG markers and fMRI targets. [Original code](https://github.com/soupeeli/NeuroBOLT); [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05341) | MR/heartbeat artifacts, synchronization, haemodynamic delays, ROI extraction, confounds and temporally dependent evaluation. | Extension, or a core only with a checked pipeline and experienced support. |

#### Reproducible projects worth reusing

- **DREAM's original feature-classification workflow:** the authors release Python analysis for experience versus no-experience prediction. Reproduce a limited part before changing the model; explicitly audit participant grouping rather than assuming the example answers unseen-person generalisation. [Original analysis release](https://zenodo.org/records/15234846); [paper code-availability statement](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12350935/)
- **MNE-Python sleep-staging tutorial:** practical fallback using spectral features and a random forest, including a documented stage-3/4 merge and exclusion of movement/unscored labels. The illustrative two-person example should be expanded to a participant-grouped cohort evaluation for honours. [Official tutorial and code](https://mne.tools/stable/auto_tutorials/clinical/60_sleep.html)
- **EEGNet via Braindecode:** optional compact neural comparator after the simple baseline. The original model/paper and original ARL implementation are available, as is a documented Braindecode implementation. Pin one implementation and its environment. A foundation-model pretraining programme is unnecessary for this scope. [Original EEGNet paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.08024); [original code](https://github.com/vlawhern/arl-eegmodels); [Braindecode EEGNet](https://braindecode.org/1.4/generated/braindecode.models.EEGNet.html)
- **NeuroBOLT reproduction:** appropriate for the paired extension, using its loader/alignment conventions and one or a few ROI targets. Its instructions explicitly keep all scans from a person in the same data partition. [Original code](https://github.com/soupeeli/NeuroBOLT); [original paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05341)

### Inferences

#### Proposed minimum project and decision gates

1. **DREAM core:** ingest the selected package; reconcile its 28 participant IDs and 308 cases with the catalogue; inspect labels, usable durations, channel names and EEG reference. Fix one window length available across retained cases. Define exclusions without looking at model outcomes. Use band-power features with logistic regression and a majority-class baseline; fit scaling and model selection using training participants only. Report balanced accuracy, AUROC where defined, a confusion matrix and uncertainty across held-out participants. Include a sleep-stage baseline or a stage-matched analysis so the report-label result can be assessed against sleep-stage differences. This is a proposed evaluation design, motivated by the subset's repeated awakenings and reported labels. [Subset documentation](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Zhang_Wamsley_2019_Final/22226692); [DREAM analysis](https://zenodo.org/records/15234846)
2. **One controlled extension to the core:** compare the same split with a small random forest/EEGNet, or compare all available EEG channels with a predefined smaller montage. Do not turn an introductory project into an architecture search. Freeze the split and preprocessing before comparing methods. [Original feature workflow](https://zenodo.org/records/15234846); [EEGNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.08024)
3. **Fallback gate:** if ingestion or label reconciliation obstructs the DREAM pilot, switch to Sleep-EDF cassette data and reproduce the MNE pipeline with person-grouped splits. Map W, 1, 2, 3/4 and R explicitly, exclude movement/unscored epochs and document wake trimming. [Sleep-EDF](https://physionet.org/content/sleep-edfx/1.0.0/sleep-telemetry/); [MNE tutorial](https://mne.tools/stable/auto_tutorials/clinical/60_sleep.html)
4. **Paired extension gate:** use ds006040 only after demonstrating EEG/BOLD trigger alignment on a run, defining a common ROI atlas and documenting nuisance handling. Start with lagged log-bandpower ridge regression and a mean-prediction baseline; then compare a NeuroBOLT result under the same split. Use participant-held-out evaluation for unseen-person claims. For within-run temporal splits, separate input windows and account for filtering/lag leakage. Report per-person/ROI correlation alongside an error metric and a time-shift negative control. These are proposed controls, not claimed published ridge results. [Paired acquisition and preprocessing](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13066628/); [NeuroBOLT input/split conventions](https://github.com/soupeeli/NeuroBOLT)

**If ds006040 must be the core:** narrow the thesis to reproducible **resting EEG-to-ROI BOLD regression**, with ridge plus one original-code comparator. Use only scanner-ON simultaneous runs as paired examples; scanner-OFF EEG has no concurrently measured BOLD. Its single session per person supports subject/task comparisons, not repeat-day reliability. Keep DREAM as a separate EEG project rather than pooling incompatible labels. [ds006040 session design and ON/OFF conditions](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13066628/)

**Team expansion, proposed:** one student owns EEG ingestion/QC and split manifests; one owns the fixed baseline/ablation; a later student owns paired alignment/ROI targets and ridge/NeuroBOLT comparison. Share versioned configurations and a locked evaluation protocol. The EEG-only core should remain a complete honours result even if the paired extension is deferred.

All suggested outcomes are **offline research results**. Report-label classification does not demonstrate dream-content decoding, and predicted ROI BOLD is a model estimate rather than a measured fMRI examination. No patient benefit, diagnostic accuracy or clinical readiness is claimed. [Actual DREAM labels](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Zhang_Wamsley_2019_Final/22226692); [released NeuroBOLT target definition](https://huggingface.co/datasets/NeurdyLab/NeuroBOLT/blob/main/README.md)

### Gaps

- No runtimes, model scores, dataset-loader success or hardware requirements were measured. Whether existing ds006040 preprocessing makes BOLD regression easier than the DREAM route remains a project-specific question for the first pilot.
- The current audit verifies public routes and documented opportunities. It does not establish that any local clinical resource is ready for an introductory student, nor that a pretrained model has never seen any proposed evaluation recordings.
